Want to pause or stop Windows 11 automatic updates fast?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the exact step-by-step method to disable them in under two minutes.
Let's jump right in!

STEP 1: DISABLE THE WINDOWS UPDATE SERVICE
* First, click on your **Start button** on the taskbar.
* Type " services " into the search box.
* Now click on the **Services** app.
* Scroll all the way down through the list.
* Locate and double-click on **Windows Update**.
* Click on the **Startup type** drop-down menu.
* Go ahead and select **Disabled**.
* And click on **Apply** to save your changes.

Now, Windows can sometimes attempt to restart disabled services in the background, so let's make sure it takes no action if it fails.

STEP 2: PREVENT THE SERVICE FROM RESTARTING
* Click on the **Recovery** tab at the top.
* Click the drop-down menu next to **First failure**.
* Select **Take No Action** from the menu.
* Now click on **Apply**.
* And click on **OK** to close the window.

Now that the service is disabled from booting up, let's stop the active update process that's currently running.

STEP 3: STOP THE ACTIVE UPDATE SERVICE
* Double-click on **Windows Update** once more.
* Under **Service status**, click right on **Stop**.
* Let's give it a few seconds to halt.
* Click on **OK** to save and exit.
* And go ahead and close the **Services** window.

Your Windows 11 updates should now be completely paused and under your total control!
