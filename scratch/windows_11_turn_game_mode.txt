STEP 1: TURN GAME MODE ON OR OFF IN SETTINGS
* First, press the windows key and " I " on your keyboard.
* Next, from the left sidebar, click on **Gaming**.
* Looking on the right, click on **Game Mode**.
* Locate the toggle switch next to **Game Mode**.
* Switch the toggle to **ON** to optimize gaming.
* Or switch it to **OFF** to disable it.
* When enabled, Windows prioritizes your hardware for games.
* And stops background updates from interrupting your match.
* And finally, close out of your **Settings** window.

And that's how you can easily turn Game Mode on or off in Windows 11!
