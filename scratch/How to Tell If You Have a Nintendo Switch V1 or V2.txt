Wondering whether you have the original Nintendo Switch V1 or the upgraded V2 model with better battery life?
In this video, I'm gonna show you two foolproof ways to verify your exact model in seconds.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: CHECK THE MODEL NUMBER ON THE BACK OF THE CONSOLE
* First, flip your Nintendo Switch console over to look at the back casing.
* Locate the small printed regulatory text directly beneath the **Nintendo Switch logo**.
* Find the label that starts with **MOD**.
* If the text displays **HAC-001**, you have the original **V1** model released in 2017.
* If the text displays **HAC-001(-01)** with a dash zero one at the end, you have the upgraded **V2** model with improved battery life.
* If you still have the original retail box, note that **V1** boxes feature a hand docking the console on a white background, while **V2** boxes have a solid red background.

If the text on the back of your console is worn down, scratched off, or covered by a protective skin, don't worry—you can check your serial number directly inside your system settings.

STEP 2: CHECK THE SERIAL NUMBER IN SYSTEM SETTINGS
* First go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** on the bottom toolbar for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
* After entering into **System**, scroll down towards the bottom and choose **Serial Information**.
* Look at the top entry listed under **Console**.
* Alternatively, check the white barcode sticker on the bottom edge next to the **USB-C port**.
* If your serial number begins with **XAW**, you have a **V1** console.
* If your serial number begins with **XKW**, you have a **V2** console.
* Press the physical **Home button** on your controller to return to your home screen.

And that's how you can easily tell whether you have a Nintendo Switch V1 or V2.
Remember, the V2 gives you almost double the battery life, while unpatched V1 models are popular for homebrew and custom software.
