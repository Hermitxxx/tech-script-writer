STEP 1: CHECK FOR CORRUPTED DATA IN SYSTEM SETTINGS
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Scroll down and click on **Manage Software**.
•  Browse through your library and choose the game giving you the corrupted data error.
•  Inside the game options, scroll down to the bottom and click on **Check for Corrupted Data**.
•  Wait a few moments while your console scans your software for damaged files.
•  If corrupted data is found, click on **Redownload** to replace damaged files without touching your save data.
•  If no corruption is found, hit **OK** to return to your menu, or choose **Redownload** if your game still crashes.
•  Check that your game now launches smoothly without any error messages.
And that's how you can check for and fix corrupted data on your Nintendo Switch.
