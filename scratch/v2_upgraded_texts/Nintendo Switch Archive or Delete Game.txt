Wondering whether you should archive or delete a game on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the exact difference and how to do both to free up storage space.
Let's jump right in!
METHOD 1: HOW TO ARCHIVE A GAME
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Highlight the game you want to archive.
•  Press the **plus button** on your right controller to open the options menu.
•  Scroll down the left menu and select **Manage Software**.
•  Move over to the right side and click on **Archive Software**.
•  Confirm by choosing **Archive**.
•  This frees up your storage space while keeping your saved data completely safe.
•  Notice that the game icon stays right on your home screen with a small cloud download icon.
•  Check that you can click on the game icon anytime to quickly redownload it.
If you want to completely remove the game and declutter your home screen, here's how to delete it.
METHOD 2: HOW TO DELETE A GAME
•  From your home screen, highlight the game you want to remove.
•  Press the **plus button** on your controller to open software options.
•  Scroll down the left menu and select **Manage Software**.
•  Move over to the right side and click on **Delete Software**.
•  Confirm by choosing **Delete** again.
•  Keep in mind that your saved data is stored separately and will not be erased.
•  Notice that both the game data and the home screen icon are now completely removed.
•  Check your home menu to verify that the icon is gone and your storage space is freed up.
And that's the difference between archiving and deleting games on your Nintendo Switch, and how to do both.
