Getting a corrupted microSD card error on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
Don't panic—in this video, I'm gonna show you 3 proven solutions to fix it without losing any of your saved data.
Let's jump right in!
SOLUTION 1: REPAIR THE MICROSD CARD USING WINDOWS
•  First, turn off your Nintendo Switch completely.
•  Open up the card slot cover and take out the microSD card.
•  Insert the microSD card into your computer using an SD card reader.
•  Press the windows key on your keyboard.
•  Type **cmd** in the search bar.
•  Right-click on **Command Prompt** from the search results and choose **Run as administrator**.
•  Click **Yes** if the User Account Control prompt appears.
•  In the command prompt window, type: **chkdsk E: /f /r**
•  Make sure to replace **E:** with the actual drive letter assigned to your microSD card in File Explorer.
•  Hit enter on your keyboard to run the scan.
•  Wait for Windows to scan the drive and repair any corrupted sectors.
•  Once the scan finishes, safely eject the microSD card from your computer.
•  Slide the microSD card back into your Nintendo Switch and power it on.
•  Check if your games and data now load without any error messages.
If Windows fails to repair the card or the drive is still unstable, let's move on to the next solution.
SOLUTION 2: MANUALLY BACK UP AND REFORMAT THE SD CARD
•  Turn off your Nintendo Switch and take out the microSD card.
•  Connect the microSD card to your computer using your card reader.
•  Open up **File Explorer** on your PC.
•  Click on your microSD card drive from the left sidebar.
•  Right-click on the folder named **Nintendo**.
•  Select **Copy**.
•  Head over to your computer's **Desktop**.
•  Right-click on any empty space and choose **Paste** to create a safe backup.
•  Once the copy finishes, safely eject the microSD card from your PC.
•  Slide the microSD card back into your Nintendo Switch and turn the console on.
•  From your home screen, move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
•  Move over to the right side, scroll down, and click on **Formatting Options**.
•  Choose **Format microSD Card** from the options.
•  Select **Continue** and confirm to format the card.
•  Wait a few moments for your Nintendo Switch to format the card and restart automatically.
•  Turn off your console again and plug the microSD card back into your computer.
•  Copy your backed-up **Nintendo** folder from your desktop.
•  Paste it directly onto the root of your freshly formatted microSD card.
•  Once the transfer is complete, safely eject the card from your PC.
•  Insert the microSD card back into your Nintendo Switch and turn it on.
•  Verify that your games and data load properly without any issues.
And that's how you can fix a corrupted microSD card on your Nintendo Switch without losing your data.
