Running out of storage space on your Nintendo Switch Lite and need room for more games?
In today’s video, I'm gonna show you the best methods to free up space on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
Let's jump right in!
METHOD 1: QUICK ARCHIVE UNPLAYED GAMES
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen and click on **Quick Archive**.
•  Browse through your installed titles and check the games you're not currently playing.
•  Move over to the right side and click on **Archive Data**.
•  Confirm by selecting **Archive**.
•  Keep in mind that your saved data is completely safe and icons remain on your home screen for easy redownload.
•  Return to your home menu and verify your newly freed storage space.
If you want to keep all your games installed, let's move them over to your microSD card.
METHOD 2: MOVE GAMES TO YOUR MICROSD CARD
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side and click on **Move Data Between System / microSD Card**.
•  Choose **Move to microSD Card** from the options.
•  Select the games or downloadable content you want to transfer.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen and click on **Move**.
•  Confirm by choosing **Move Data** to begin transferring.
•  Wait a few moments for the data to copy over to your card.
•  Check your system memory to verify that internal storage space has been cleared.
If you still need extra space, let's clear out large captured videos and screenshots.
METHOD 3: DELETE CAPTURED VIDEOS AND SCREENSHOTS
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the blue **Album** icon on the bottom row.
•  Filter by **Videos Only** to locate your largest capture files.
•  Press the **X button** on your controller to open management options.
•  Check the video clips you no longer need.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen and click on **Delete**.
•  Confirm by choosing **Delete** again.
•  Return to your home menu and check that your available storage space has increased.
And that's how you can easily free up storage space on your Nintendo Switch.
