Want to delete screenshots and videos taking up space on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to delete multiple files or wipe them all at once in just a few simple steps.
So, Let's jump right in!
METHOD 1: DELETE MULTIPLE SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS AT ONCE
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the blue **Album** icon on the bottom row.
•  Inside the album, press the **X button** on your controller to open the manage menu.
•  Scroll through your captures and choose the pictures or videos you want to remove.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Click on **Delete**.
•  Confirm by choosing **Delete** again.
•  Check your album gallery to verify that all selected captures have been cleared.
If you have hundreds of captures and want to wipe all of them at once, let's delete them through System Settings.
METHOD 2: DELETE ALL SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS AT ONCE VIA SYSTEM SETTINGS
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Scroll down and click on **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
•  Choose your storage location by picking either **System Memory** or **microSD Card**.
•  Scroll down and select **Delete All Screenshots and Videos**.
•  Confirm the permanent removal by selecting **Delete Screenshots and Videos**.
•  Check your storage screen to verify that your capture space has been completely freed up.
And that's how you can easily delete screenshots and videos on your Nintendo Switch.
