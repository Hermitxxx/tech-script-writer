STEP 1: PREVENT AUTO-ARCHIVING IN DATA MANAGEMENT
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Scroll down and click on **Manage Software**.
•  Browse through your library and choose the specific game you want to protect from archiving.
•  Inside the game settings, scroll down and select **Prevent Auto-Archiving**.
•  Switch the toggle over here to **Yes** to keep the game permanently installed.
•  Repeat this step for any other games you don't want the console to automatically touch.
•  Return to your home menu and check that your protected games stay fully installed on your console.
And that's how you can prevent auto-archiving on your Nintendo Switch.
