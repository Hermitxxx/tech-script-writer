If your microSD card isn't being recognized or working properly on your Nintendo Switch Lite, then I'm gonna show you how to resolve this issue in just a few simple steps.
SOLUTION 1: SAFELY RE-INSERT AND RESTART YOUR CONSOLE
•  First, press and hold the physical power button on top of your Switch for three seconds.
•  Choose **Power Options** from the popup menu.
•  Select **Turn Off** to shut down your console completely.
•  Open up the card slot cover on the back of your console.
•  Gently remove the microSD card from the slot.
•  Check the golden contact pins on the card for any dust or debris.
•  Slide the microSD card firmly back into the slot until it clicks.
•  Press the power button to turn your Nintendo Switch back **ON**.
•  From your home screen, move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Look over to the right side of the screen to check if your **microSD Card** is detected.
If your Switch still doesn't recognize the card, let's check for a missing system update.
SOLUTION 2: INSTALL THE LATEST SYSTEM UPDATE
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Click on **System Update**.
•  If a new update is available, select **Update** to begin downloading.
•  Wait a few moments for the console to install the update and restart automatically.
•  Once your console reboots, head into **Data Management** in settings and check if your storage appears.
If your microSD card is still not working, let's reformat the card directly on your Switch.
SOLUTION 3: FORMAT THE MICROSD CARD ON NINTENDO SWITCH LITE
•  From your home screen, move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
•  Move over to the right side and scroll down to **Formatting Options**.
•  Click on **Formatting Options**.
•  Choose **Format microSD Card**.
•  Review the warning on screen and select **Continue**.
•  Confirm by choosing **Format** to erase the card and rebuild the file system.
•  Wait a few moments for the console to restart automatically.
•  Head back into **Data Management** to verify that your microSD card is now active and ready to use.
And that's how you can fix a microSD card not working on your Nintendo Switch.
