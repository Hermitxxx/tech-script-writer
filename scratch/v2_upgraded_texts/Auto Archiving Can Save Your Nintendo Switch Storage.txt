Running low on storage space on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In today’s video, I'm gonna show you how auto-archiving can automatically save your storage without losing any save data.
Let's jump right in!
STEP 1: ENABLE AUTO-ARCHIVING IN DATA MANAGEMENT
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Scroll down and select **Manage Software**.
•  Browse through your library and choose the specific game or app you want the console to manage.
•  Inside the game settings, click on **Prevent Auto-Archiving**.
•  Switch the option to **No** to turn off archiving prevention.
•  Check that your Switch will now automatically archive unplayed software whenever your console runs low on space.
•  Keep in mind that your saved data is stored separately and will never be touched.
And that's how enabling auto-archiving can save your storage space on your Nintendo Switch.
