Did you just run out of internal memory on your Nintendo Switch Lite?

STEP 1: MOVE GAMES TO MICROSD CARD IN DATA MANAGEMENT
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Click on **Move Data Between System / microSD Card**.
•  Choose **Move to microSD Card** from the options.
•  Browse through your library and check the box next to any games or apps you want to move.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen and click on **Move Data**.
•  Review the confirmation prompt showing the transfer size.
•  Confirm by choosing **Move** to begin transferring.
•  Wait a few moments while the progress bar copies the game data over to your card.
•  Hit **OK** once the transfer finishes.
•  Return to your home screen and launch your transferred game to check that it plays smoothly.
And that's how you can quickly transfer Nintendo Switch games and apps to your microSD card.
