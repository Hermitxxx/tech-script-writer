STEP 1: COPY SCREENSHOTS TO MICROSD CARD IN DATA MANAGEMENT
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Scroll down and click on **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
•  Under storage options, choose **System Memory**.
•  Click on **Copy All Screenshots and Videos to microSD Card**.
•  Confirm the transfer by selecting **Copy** on the popup prompt.
•  Wait a few moments while the progress bar copies the files over to your card.
•  Hit **OK** once the transfer finishes.
•  Check your storage screen to verify that all your captures are now copied onto your card.
And that's how you can easily move screenshots to your microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
