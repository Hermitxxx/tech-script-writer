STEP 1: TURN OFF AUTOMATIC SOFTWARE UPDATES
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Scroll down until you find **Auto-Update Software**.
•  Switch the setting to **Off**.
•  This prevents your games from automatically downloading updates in the background or during sleep mode.
•  Check that your setting is now active by returning to your home screen.
And that's how you can disable automatic game updates on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
