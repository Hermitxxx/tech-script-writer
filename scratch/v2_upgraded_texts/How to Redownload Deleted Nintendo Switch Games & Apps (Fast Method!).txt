STEP 1: REDOWNLOAD VIA VIRTUAL GAME CARDS
•  First turn on your Nintendo Switch and make sure you're connected to Wi-Fi.
•  From your home screen, move over to the **Virtual Game Cards** icon on the bottom row.
•  Choose the user profile linked to your Nintendo Account.
•  Scroll through your library and highlight the deleted game or app.
•  Click on the game card to open its options.
•  Choose **Load on this system** from the menu.
•  Wait a few moments while your Switch authenticates your license and installs the game files.
•  Return to your home menu and check that your game is now downloaded and ready to play.
