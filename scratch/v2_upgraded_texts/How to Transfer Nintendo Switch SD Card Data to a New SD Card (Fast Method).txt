Upgrading to a bigger memory card for your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to transfer all your existing SD card data to a new SD card quickly and safely without losing anything.
Let's jump right in!
STEP 1: COPY THE NINTENDO FOLDER TO YOUR COMPUTER
•  First, press and hold the physical power button on top of your Switch for three seconds.
•  Choose **Power Options** from the popup menu.
•  Select **Turn Off** to shut down your console completely.
•  Open up the card slot cover on your console.
•  Gently remove the microSD card.
•  Insert your old microSD card into your computer using an SD card reader.
•  Open up **File Explorer** on your PC.
•  Click on your microSD card drive from the left sidebar.
•  Right-click on the folder named **Nintendo**.
•  Select **Copy**.
•  Head over to your computer's **Desktop**.
•  Right-click on any empty space and choose **Paste**.
•  Wait for the **Nintendo** folder to finish copying completely to your PC.
•  Once the copy finishes, safely eject the old microSD card from your computer.
Once the files are saved on your computer, let's move them onto your new SD card.
STEP 2: TRANSFER THE NINTENDO FOLDER TO YOUR NEW SD CARD
•  Insert your new microSD card into your computer's card reader.
•  Open up **File Explorer** on your PC.
•  Click on your new microSD card drive from the left sidebar.
•  Make sure the card is empty or format it first if needed.
•  Head back to your **Desktop** where you saved your backup folder.
•  Right-click on the **Nintendo** folder.
•  Select **Copy**.
•  Navigate back to your new microSD card drive in **File Explorer**.
•  Right-click on an empty space inside the drive and choose **Paste**.
•  Wait for the entire **Nintendo** folder to finish copying over to your new card.
•  Once the transfer is complete, safely eject your new microSD card from your PC.
Now that your data is transferred, let's put the new card into your Switch and verify everything works.
STEP 3: INSERT THE NEW SD CARD AND VERIFY YOUR GAMES
•  Slide your new microSD card firmly into the slot on your Nintendo Switch until it clicks.
•  Press the power button to turn your console back on.
•  From your home screen, move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Check the **microSD Card** section on the right to verify your new storage space.
•  Launch one of your installed games to confirm that all your data loads properly.
And that's how you can transfer your Nintendo Switch SD card data to a new SD card.
