Getting the not enough free space to download the data error on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
Don't worry—in this video, I'm gonna show you 3 quick solutions to free up gigabytes of storage without losing any saved data.
Let's jump right in!
SOLUTION 1: QUICK ARCHIVE UNPLAYED GAMES
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen and click on **Quick Archive**.
•  Browse through your installed titles and check the box next to any games you're not currently playing.
•  Move over to the right side and click on **Archive Data**.
•  Confirm by choosing **Archive**.
•  Keep in mind that your save data remains completely safe and icons stay on your home screen for easy redownload.
•  Return to your home screen and check that your download resumes normally.
If you want to keep all your games installed, let's move data to your microSD card.
SOLUTION 2: MOVE DATA TO YOUR MICROSD CARD
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
•  Move over to the right side and click on **Move Data Between System / microSD Card**.
•  Choose **Move to microSD Card** from the options.
•  Check the boxes next to the games or DLC you want to transfer.
•  Move over to the right side and click on **Move**.
•  Confirm by selecting **Move Data** to begin transferring files.
•  Wait a few moments for the data to copy over to your card.
•  Check your system memory to verify that internal storage space has been freed up.
If you still don't have enough room, let's clear out large captured videos and screenshots.
SOLUTION 3: DELETE CAPTURED SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the blue **Album** icon on the bottom row.
•  Press the **X button** on your controller to open management options.
•  Check the boxes next to all the video clips or screenshots you no longer need.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen and click on **Delete**.
•  Confirm by selecting **Delete** again.
•  Return to your home screen and check that your game download now proceeds without error.
And that's how you can fix not enough free space to download data on your Nintendo Switch.
