If you want to stop your Nintendo Switch from making notification sounds or turn them back on, I'm gonna show you the easiest way to do it in seconds.
Let's get right into it!
STEP 1: ADJUST NOTIFICATION SOUNDS IN SYSTEM SETTINGS
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Notifications**.
•  Move over to the right side of the screen.
•  Click on **Notification Sound**.
•  Switch the setting to **Off** if you want complete silence from notifications.
•  Or choose **On** if you want to hear chime alerts whenever notifications arrive.
•  Check that your notification sounds now behave exactly the way you prefer.
And that is basically how you control notification sounds on your Nintendo Switch.
