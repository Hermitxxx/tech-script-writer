In today's video, I'm gonna show you how to resolve Virtual Game Cards missing on your Nintendo Switch Lite in just a few simple steps.
Let's get right into it!
SOLUTION 1: DEREGISTER CONSOLE IN NINTENDO ESHOP
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the orange **Nintendo eShop** icon on the bottom row.
•  Choose the user profile linked to your Nintendo Account.
•  Move up to the top right corner and click on your **Profile Icon** to open Account Information.
•  Scroll down the left menu and select **Settings**.
•  Move over to the right panel and scroll down to locate **Primary Console**.
•  Click on **Deregister**.
•  Enter your Nintendo Account password if prompted for security.
•  Confirm by selecting **Deregister** again.
•  Press the physical **Home button** to exit back to your home screen.
•  Reopen **Nintendo eShop** to automatically re-sync your account and license status.
•  Return to your home menu and check if your virtual game cards start appearing.
If your virtual game cards are still not showing up, let's clear out corrupted temporary cache data.
SOLUTION 2: CLEAR SYSTEM CACHE FOR YOUR USER PROFILE
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
•  Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
•  Move over to the right side, scroll all the way down, and click on **Formatting Options**.
•  Choose **Clear Cache** from the list of options.
•  Select your primary user profile.
•  Confirm by choosing **Reset** to clear temporary cache files and saved IDs.
•  Check on screen that your cache data has been successfully cleared.
Now that the cache has been reset, let's perform a full system reboot to reload your game licenses.
SOLUTION 3: RESTART YOUR CONSOLE TO RE-INDEX LICENSES
•  Press and hold the physical power button on top of your console for three seconds.
•  Choose **Power Options** from the popup menu.
•  Click on **Restart**.
•  Wait a few moments for your Nintendo Switch to reboot completely.
•  Make sure your console connects to the internet after restarting.
•  Open up the **Virtual Game Cards** menu from your home screen.
•  Verify that all of your missing virtual game cards have now been restored to your screen.
And that's how you can fix missing virtual game cards on your Nintendo Switch after an update.
