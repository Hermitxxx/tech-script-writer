Need to redownload archived or deleted games on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'll show you how to get them back in just a few simple taps without paying twice.
Let's jump right in!
METHOD 1: REDOWNLOAD ARCHIVED GAMES
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the **Virtual Game Cards** icon on the bottom row.
•  Choose the user profile that originally purchased the game.
•  Scroll through your digital card library and highlight your archived title.
•  Click on the game card to open its details.
•  Choose **Load on this system** to initiate the download.
•  Wait a few moments for your license to authenticate with Nintendo's servers.
•  Return to your home screen and check that the game icon has reappeared and started installing.
If you completely deleted the game and removed its icon from your console, let's redownload it from the Nintendo eShop.
METHOD 2: REDOWNLOAD COMPLETELY DELETED GAMES FROM THE NINTENDO ESHOP
•  First go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the orange **Nintendo eShop** icon on the bottom row.
•  Select the user profile linked to your Nintendo Account purchase.
•  Move up to the top right corner and click on your **Profile Icon** to open Account Information.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Redownload**.
•  Browse through your purchase history to locate your deleted game.
•  Hit the orange **Download Cloud** icon next to the game's title.
•  Return to your home screen and verify that the game is downloading and ready to play.
And that's how you can redownload archived and deleted games on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
