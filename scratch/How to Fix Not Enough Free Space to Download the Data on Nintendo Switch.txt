Are you Getting the "not enough free space to download the data" error on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
Don’t worry --- In today’s video, I’m gonna show you three quick solutions to free up gigabytes of storage without losing your saved data.
Let's jump right in!
SOLUTION 1: QUICK ARCHIVE UNPLAYED GAMES
•  First, go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for " System Settings ".
•  After opening system settings, scroll down and select " Data Management ".
Then select " Quick Archive " and open it.
A list of downloaded games on your console will appear on the screen.
•  Check the box next to any games you are not currently playing.
•  Review the total free space that will be created.
•  Select " Archive Data " and click on " Archive " to confirm.
•  Finally, return to your home screen and check if your download starts normally.
If you want to keep all your games installed, let's move data to your microSD card.
SOLUTION 2: MOVE DATA TO YOUR MICROSD CARD
•  First, go to your home screen.
•  Then, move over to the gear icon for " System Settings ".
•  Scroll down on the left side of the screen and select " Data Management ".
•  After that move over to the right and open " Move Data Between System / microSD Card ".
•  Select " Move to microSD Card " from the options that will appear.
•  Choose the game or downloadable content you want to transfer.
•  Select " Move " and then click on " Move Data " to start the transfer.
•  Wait a few moments for the data transfer to complete.
•  Finally, check that your system memory now has enough free space available.
If you still don't have enough room, let's clear out large captured videos and screenshots.
SOLUTION 3: DELETE CAPTURED SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS
•  First, go to your home screen.
•  Open the " Album " app from the bottom toolbar.
•  Select " Filter " on the right or press the " Y " button on your controller.
•  Choose " Videos Only " to locate your largest capture files.
•  Press the " X " button on your controller to open the manage menu.
•  Select the video clips you no longer need.
•  Delete the selected items by clicking on " Delete ".
•  Finally, resume your download from the home screen.
And that's how you can fix not enough free space to download data on your Nintendo Switch.
