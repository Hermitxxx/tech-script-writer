METHOD 1: QUICK ARCHIVE UNPLAYED GAMES

•  Move over to the gear icon for " System Settings ".
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select " Data Management ".

•  Select games you are not currently playing.

METHOD 2: MOVE GAMES TO YOUR MICROSD CARD
•  First, open " System Settings " from your HOME Menu.
•  Scroll down on the left and select " Data Management " .
•  Select " Move Data Between System / microSD Card " .
•  After that select " Move to microSD Card " for transferring games to your microSD card.
•  Select the games or downloadable content you want to transfer.
•  Select " Move " on the right.
•  Select " Move Data " to start the transfer.
•  Wait a few moments for the data to copy over to your card.
•  Finally, check your system memory to verify that internal storage space has been cleared.
If you still need extra space, let's clear out large captured videos and screenshots.
METHOD 3: DELETE CAPTURED VIDEOS AND SCREENSHOTS
•  First, return to your HOME Menu.
•  Open the " Album " app from the bottom toolbar.
•  Press the " Y " button on your controller to open the filter menu.
•  Choose " Videos Only " to find your largest capture files.
•  Press the " X " button on your controller to open management options.
•  Check the boxes next to all the video clips you no longer need.
•  Hit the " Delete " button on the right.
•  Select " Delete " again to confirm the deletion.
•  Finally, return to your HOME Menu and check your available system storage.
