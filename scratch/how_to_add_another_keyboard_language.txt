Want to add a new keyboard language and switch layouts on Windows 11 fast?

In this video, I'll show you the exact step-by-step method to install any language and switch between them instantly, so let's jump right in!

STEP 1: ADD A NEW KEYBOARD LANGUAGE
•  First, right-click on your **Start button**.
•  From the menu, click right on **Settings**.
•  In the sidebar, select **Time & language**.
•  Next, click directly on **Language & region**.
•  Beside Preferred languages, click **Add a language**.
•  In the search box, type your language.
•  Select your language from the search results.
•  Down at the bottom, click on **Next**.
•  Now, click right on **Install**.
•  Give Windows a few moments to install.

Now that your new language is installed, let's look at how to quickly switch between keyboards while typing.

STEP 2: SWITCH BETWEEN KEYBOARD LANGUAGES
•  Look at the bottom-right of your taskbar.
•  Click on your **language abbreviation** icon.
•  From the list, select your new keyboard.
•  Alternatively, hold down the **Windows key**.
•  Tap the **Spacebar** to cycle between languages.
•  Your keyboard is now ready for typing.
•  Finally, close out of your **Settings** app.

And that's how you can easily add another keyboard language on Windows 11!
