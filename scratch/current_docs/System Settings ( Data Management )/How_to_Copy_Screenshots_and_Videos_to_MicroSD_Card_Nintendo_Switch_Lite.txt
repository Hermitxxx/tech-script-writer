Want to free up internal storage on your Nintendo Switch Lite by moving all your game captures to an SD card?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to copy all your screenshots and videos to a microSD card in seconds.
Let's get right into it
* From your home screen, move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar.
* Select **Data Management**.
* Move over to the right side of the screen.
* Scroll down and select **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* Select **System Memory**.
* Select **Copy All Screenshots and Videos to microSD Card**.
* Wait for the progress bar to finish.
* Select **OK** once the copy is complete.
* Check your storage to verify that all your captures are now safely on your microSD card.
And that's how you can copy all your screenshots and videos to a microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
