Want to quickly transfer your Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots directly to your Android phone?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to do it in seconds using the built-in QR code method.
Let's get right into it!
STEP 1: CHOOSE SCREENSHOTS ON NINTENDO SWITCH**
* First go to your Nintendo Switch home screen.
* Move to the bottom row and **s****elect** the blue **Album** icon.
* Open the screenshot you want to transfer to your phone.
* Press “ **A** “ on your controller.
* Click on **Send to Smartphone**.
* Leave the first **QR code** open on your Switch screen.
Now grab your Android phone so we can connect to your Switch and download your photos.
STEP 2: SCAN THE QR CODES AND DOWNLOAD ON ANDROID**
* Open up the **Camera** app on your Android phone.
* Point your camera at the first **QR code** on your Switch screen.
* Tap on the network prompt that pops up and hit **Connect**.
* Once your phone connects to the Switch, point your camera at the second **QR code**.
* Tap on the website link that appears on your phone screen.
* In your browser, press and hold the screenshot and choose **Download image**.
* Check that your screenshot now appears inside your phone's **Gallery** or **Photos** app.
* Back on your Switch, select **End** to close the connection.
And that's how you can easily copy your Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots to your Android phone.
