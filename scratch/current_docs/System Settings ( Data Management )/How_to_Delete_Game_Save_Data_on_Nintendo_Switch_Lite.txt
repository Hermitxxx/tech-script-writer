Want to delete old game save data or start completely fresh on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to safely delete game save data in just a few simple steps.
Let's get right into it!
* First go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* Move over to the right side of the screen.
* Scroll all the way down to the bottom and click on **Delete Save Data**.
* Choose the game whose save data you want to remove.
* Pick **Delete Save Data for [User]**, or choose **Delete All Save Data for This Software**.
* Confirm by clicking on **Delete Save Data** on the warning prompt.
* Wait a moment for the process to finish, then hit **OK**.
* Check that your game save data is now completely cleared from your console.
* Press the physical **Home button** on your Nintendo Switch Lite to head back to your home screen.
And that's how you can delete game save data on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
