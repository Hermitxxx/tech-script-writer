Want your new Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots and gameplay videos to automatically save to your SD card?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to change your default save location in seconds.
Let's get right into it!
* First go to your home screen.
* Move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* Then scroll down and go to **Manage Screenshots and Videos** on the right menu.
* Click on **Save Location**.
* After that select **microSD Card** from the popup menu.
* Check that your save location now displays **microSD Card**.
And that's how you can set your default capture save location to your microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
