Want to transfer your Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots and gameplay videos over to your PC?
Well, I'm gonna show you the quickest and easiest ways to copy all of your clips in just a couple of minutes.
Let's get right into it!
STEP 1: TRANSFER VIA USB CABLE IN SYSTEM SETTINGS**
* From your Nintendo Switch home screen, move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar.
* Select **Data Management**.
* Move over to the right menu.
* Scroll down and select **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* Select **Copy to a Computer via USB Connection**.
* Plug one end of a USB-C cable into the bottom of your Switch.
* Plug the other end into a USB port on your PC.
* Check your Switch screen until it says **Connected to the computer**.
* Now, head over to your computer.
* Open up **File Explorer** on your PC.
* Click on **This PC** from the left sidebar.
* Double-click on **Nintendo Switch** under your drives.
* Open the **Album** folder.
* Browse into the specific game folder you want to copy from.
* Select the screenshots or video clips you want.
* Copy and paste them into your preferred folder on your computer.
* When your transfer is finished, look back at your Switch screen.
* Select **Disconnect**.
* Unplug the USB cable from your console.
If your computer isn't recognizing the USB connection or you don't have a compatible data cable, you can also transfer your files directly using your microSD card.
STEP 2: TRANSFER FILES USING A MICROSD CARD**
* Hold down the **power button** on top of your Switch for about three seconds.
* Select **Power Options** from the popup menu.
* Select **Turn Off**.
* Flip out the kickstand on the back of your console.
* Gently press and remove your **microSD card**.
* Insert the microSD card into your computer's card reader.
* Open **File Explorer** on your PC.
* Click on your **microSD card** drive from the left menu.
* Open the **Nintendo** folder.
* Open the **Album** folder.
* Browse into the specific game folder you want to copy from.
* Select the screenshots or video clips you want.
* Copy and paste them into your preferred folder on your computer.
* When you're done, safely eject the microSD card from your computer.
* Slide the card back into your Nintendo Switch.
And that's how you can easily copy your screenshots and videos from your Nintendo Switch Lite to your PC.
