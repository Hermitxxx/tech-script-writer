Want to copy all your screenshots and videos from your SD card straight to your Nintendo Switch Lite memory?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to transfer everything in just a few simple steps.
Let's get right into it!
* First up, go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* Then go to **Manage Screenshots and Videos** on the right menu.
* Select **microSD Card** from the left menu.
* Select **Copy All Screenshots and Videos to System Memory**.
* Wait for the progress bar to finish copying all your files.
* Select **OK** once the transfer is complete.
And that's how you can copy all your SD card screenshots and videos to your Nintendo Switch Lite memory.
