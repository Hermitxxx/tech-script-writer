Need to quickly clear out all the screenshots and videos on your Nintendo Switch Lite to free up storage?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to delete all your captures in just a few simple steps.
Let's jump right in!
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And open up **System Settings** from the bottom toolbar.
* After that, scroll down on the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* Once you're inside **Data Management**, scroll down and go to **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* Select your storage location, choosing either **System Memory** or **microSD Card**.
* Scroll down on the left and select **Delete All Screenshots and Videos**.
* Select **Delete Screenshots and Videos** to confirm the permanent removal of all captures.
* Finally, check your storage screen to verify that your capture space has been completely freed.
And that's how you can delete all screenshots and videos on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
