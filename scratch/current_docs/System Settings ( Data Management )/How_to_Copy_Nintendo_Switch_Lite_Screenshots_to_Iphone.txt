Want to quickly transfer your Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots directly to your Iphone?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to do it in just a few simple steps.
STEP 1: CHOOSE SCREENSHOTS ON NINTENDO SWITCH**
* First, go to your console’s home screen.
* Then open **Album** from the bottom toolbar.
* Open the screenshot you want to transfer to your phone.
* Press “ **A** “ on your controller.
* Click on **Send to Smartphone** from the left menu.
* Leave the first **QR code** open on your Switch screen.
Now grab your Iphone so we can connect to your Switch and download your photos.
STEP 2: SCAN THE QR CODES AND DOWNLOAD ON ****IPHONE**
* Open up the **Camera** app on your Iphone.
* Point your camera at the first **QR code** on your Switch screen.
* Tap on the network prompt that pops up and hit **Connect**.
* Once your phone connects to the Switch, point your camera at the second **QR code**.
* Tap on the website link that appears on your phone screen.
* In your browser, press the blue **Download** button under the photo or video**.**
* Check that your screenshot now appears inside your phone's **Photos** app.
* Get back on your Switch, select **End** to close the connection.
And that's how you can easily copy your Nintendo Switch Lite’s screenshots to your Iphone.
