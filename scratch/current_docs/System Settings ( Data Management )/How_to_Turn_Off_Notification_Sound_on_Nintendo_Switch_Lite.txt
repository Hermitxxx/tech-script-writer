Want to stop your Nintendo Switch Lite from making chime sounds every time you get a notification?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to turn off your notification sounds in seconds.
So, let's get right into it!
* First, go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** on for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and **Go** to **Notifications**.
* Then scroll down on the right menu to find the **Notification Sound** option.
* Switch the toggle to **OFF** to silence all notification chimes.
* Check that the setting now displays **OFF**.
This will stop your Nintendo Switch Lite from making chime sounds every time a notification arrives.
