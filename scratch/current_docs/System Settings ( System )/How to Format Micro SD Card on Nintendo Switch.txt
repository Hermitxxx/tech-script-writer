In this video, I'm gonna show you how to properly format microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite directly from system settings in just a few clicks.
So, Let's get right into it!
* From your home screen, move over to the gear icon for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
* Then scroll to the very bottom on the right menu and **G****o** to **Formatting Options**.
* Click on **Formatting Options**.
* Choose **Format microSD Card**.
* Review the warning on screen and select **Continue**.
* Confirm by choosing **Format** to erase the card and rebuild the file system.
* Wait a few moments for the console to restart automatically.
* Head back into **Data Management** from** System Settings** to verify that your microSD card is now active and ready to use.
So, that's how you can properly format a microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
