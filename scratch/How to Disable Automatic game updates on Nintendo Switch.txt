Are you tired of unexpected game updates taking up your internet bandwidth or delaying your gameplay?
Don’t worry – today I am gonna show you how you can pause automatic game updates on your Nintendo switch lite.
•  First, from your HOME Menu, open " System Settings " from the bottom toolbar.
•  Scroll all the way down on the left side and select " System " .
•  Scroll down on the right side until you find " Auto-Update Software " .
•  Select it to toggle the setting to " Off " .
•  This prevents games from downloading updates automatically in the background or during sleep mode.
•  Finally, return to your HOME Menu to confirm your new setting is active.
And that's how you can disable automatic game updates on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
