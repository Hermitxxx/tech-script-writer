Need to access Safe Mode in Windows 11 even if your PC won't start?

In this video, I'll show you how to easily boot into Safe Mode both from Settings and when your computer won't boot at all, so let's get right into it!

STEP 1: BOOT INTO SAFE MODE FROM SETTINGS
•  First, right-click on your **Start button**.
•  Next, click right on **Settings**.
•  From the left menu, click on **System**.
•  Now scroll down and select **Recovery**.
•  Looking down the list, find **Advanced startup**.
•  Right beside it, click on **Restart now**.
•  When prompted, click on **Restart now** again.
•  Give your computer a few moments to restart.
•  On the blue screen, click on **Troubleshoot**.
•  From here, select **Advanced options**.
•  Next up, click on **Startup Settings**.
•  Down at the bottom, click on **Restart**.
•  Now press the number " 4 " on your keyboard.
•  Finally, let your PC boot into **Safe Mode**.

If your computer won't boot into Windows at all, don't worry—follow this next step to trigger Safe Mode directly from startup.

STEP 2: BOOT INTO SAFE MODE WHEN WINDOWS WON'T START
•  First, press the **power button** to start your PC.
•  As the logo appears, hold down the **power button**.
•  Keep holding it until your computer completely powers off.
•  Next, press the **power button** to turn it back on.
•  As it starts loading, hold the **power button** again.
•  Repeat this one more time to trigger three forced shutdowns.
•  Now turn your computer on and let it load.
•  When the blue screen appears, click on **Advanced options**.
•  On the next menu, click on **Troubleshoot**.
•  From here, select **Advanced options**.
•  Next up, click on **Startup Settings**.
•  Down at the bottom, click on **Restart**.
•  Now press the number " 4 " on your keyboard.
•  Finally, let your PC boot into **Safe Mode**.

If your PC still won't boot even in Safe Mode, you may need to use a bootable USB recovery drive to repair your installation.
