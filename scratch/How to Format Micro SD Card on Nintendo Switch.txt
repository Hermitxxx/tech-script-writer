Need to wipe your microSD card or prepare a brand new memory card for your Nintendo Switch?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to properly format your card directly in system settings in just a few clicks.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: FORMAT MICROSD CARD IN SYSTEM SETTINGS
* First go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** on the bottom toolbar for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
* After entering into **System**, scroll down to the very bottom and choose **Formatting Options**.
* Enter your PIN if you have **Parental Controls** turned on.
* Once inside **Formatting Options**, scroll down and click on **Format microSD Card**.
* Read the warning prompt on your screen and choose **Continue**.
* Keep in mind that formatting will delete all downloaded software and screenshots on the card, but your game save progress stays safe on your console memory.
* Confirm the format by hitting **Format**.
* Wait a few moments while your console formats the card and restarts automatically.

Once your console powers back up to your home screen, let's verify that your storage is recognized and set your captures to save directly to your clean SD card.

STEP 2: VERIFY STORAGE SPACE AND SET SAVE LOCATION
* From your home screen, head back into the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* Check the right side of your screen to verify that your **microSD Card** displays clean, available storage space.
* After entering into **Data Management**, scroll down and click on **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* Once inside **Manage Screenshots and Videos**, choose **Save Location**.
* Switch your save location to **microSD Card**.
* Check that your save location now displays **microSD Card**.
* Press the physical **Home button** on your controller to return to your home screen.

And that's how you can properly format a microSD card on your Nintendo Switch.
