Are you getting ready to sell, trade in, or give away your Nintendo Switch and need to wipe everything clean?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to delete all data in your system memory and restore factory settings.

STEP 1: INITIALIZE CONSOLE AND WIPE SYSTEM MEMORY IN SETTINGS
* First go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** on the bottom toolbar for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
* After entering into **System**, scroll down to the very bottom and choose **Formatting Options**.
* Enter your PIN if you have **Parental Controls** turned on.
* Once inside **Formatting Options**, scroll to the bottom and click on **Initialize Console**.
* Review the warning screen explaining that all save data, screenshots, and user accounts will be permanently wiped from system memory.
* Click on **Next** to proceed.
* Note that your console must remain connected to the internet during this step so it can safely unlink your Nintendo Account.
* Confirm the system memory wipe by selecting **Initialize**.
* Wait a few minutes for the progress bar to complete and allow the console to restart.
* Verify that your screen powers back on with the initial out-of-the-box language setup prompt.

If your console is frozen, stuck in a boot loop, or you can't reach your home menu to open settings, you can wipe your system memory directly using physical buttons in Recovery Mode.

STEP 2: WIPE SYSTEM MEMORY DIRECTLY FROM RECOVERY MODE
* Press and hold the physical **POWER Button** on the top edge of your console for three seconds.
* Select **Power Options** from the popup menu.
* Click on **Turn Off**.
* If your console is completely frozen or unresponsive, hold down the physical **POWER Button** for twelve seconds to force a complete shutdown.
* Now press and hold down both the physical **Volume Up button** and **Volume Down button** at the same time.
* While keeping both volume buttons held down, press the physical **POWER Button** once.
* Continue holding both volume buttons until the **Maintenance Mode** recovery screen appears.
* Once inside the recovery menu, scroll down and select **Initialize Console**.
* Review the on-screen notice and confirm by choosing **Initialize**.
* Wait a few minutes for the system wipe to complete, and verify that your console restarts to the fresh initial setup screen.

And that's how you can delete all data in your system memory and completely reset your Nintendo Switch.
