Need to convert video files on Windows 11 without any watermarks or hassle?

In this video, I'll show you the fastest free method right inside your browser without installing any software, so let's get right into it!

STEP 1: UPLOAD YOUR VIDEO TO CLOUDCONVERT
•  First, open up your web browser.
•  Head over to the website **cloudconvert.com**.
•  Click on the red **Select File** button.
•  Browse and select your video file.
•  Down at the bottom, click on **Open**.

Now that your video is uploaded, let's select your output format and start the conversion.

STEP 2: SELECT FORMAT AND CONVERT
•  Beside your file, click **Select Format**.
•  From the menu, hover over **Video**.
•  Select your desired format like **MP4**.
•  Click the **wrench icon** for settings.
•  Down at the bottom-right, click **Convert**.
•  Give the site a moment to process.
•  Finally, click the green **Download** button.
•  Your converted video is saved to your PC.

Your converted video should now play smoothly on any device or media player!
