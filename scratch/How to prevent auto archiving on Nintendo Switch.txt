Want to stop your Nintendo Switch Lite from automatically archiving software?
Let me guide you through some quick steps on how to do it.
•  First, go to your HOME screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for " System Settings ".
•  Then, scroll down the left menu and select " Data Management " .
•  On the right side of the screen, select " Manage Software " .
•  Find and select the specific game you want to protect from being archived.
•  Highlight " Prevent Auto-Archiving " .
•  Toggle the setting to " On " to keep the game permanently installed.
•  Repeat this process for every individual game you do not want the console to automatically archive.
•  Finally, return to your HOME Menu and verify that your protected games stay on your console.
And that's how you can prevent auto-archiving on your Nintendo Switch.
