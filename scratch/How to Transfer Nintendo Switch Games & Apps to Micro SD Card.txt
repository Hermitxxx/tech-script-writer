Did you just run out of internal memory on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
Don’t worry --- In today’s video, I’m gonna show you the fastest way to transfer your games and apps directly to your microSD card.
•  First of all, go to your home screen.
•  Move over to the gear icon for " System Settings " .
•  Scroll down until you reach " Data Management " .
•  Select " Data Management " .
•  Look over on the right side and select " Move Data Between System / microSD Card " .
•  Select " Move to microSD Card " from the options that will appear on the screen.
•  Now, browse the list of games and apps installed on your internal memory.
•  Check the box next to any game you want to move over to your memory card.
•  After choosing your games, select " Move Data" on the right side of the screen.
•  Review the confirmation prompt showing the total transfer size.
•  Select " Move " to begin the transfer.
•  Wait patiently while the progress bar copies the game data over to your microSD card.
•  Once the transfer finishes, select " OK ".
•  Finally, return to your home screen and launch your transferred game to make sure it plays smoothly.
And that's how you can quickly transfer Nintendo Switch games and apps to your microSD card.
