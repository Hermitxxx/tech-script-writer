If you need to take a screenshot on Windows 11, then in this video I'm gonna show you every single way to capture your screen quickly. So, let's get right into it!

STEP 1: USE THE PRINT SCREEN KEY (SNIPPING TOOL)
•  First, press the **PrtScn** key on your keyboard.
•  A small toolbar will appear at the top.
•  Choose the **Rectangle mode** icon to snip.
•  Now, click and drag over the desired area.
•  Release your mouse to capture the screenshot.
•  Click on the notification banner that pops up.
•  From here, click **Save** in the top-right.

If you want to instantly save a full-screen screenshot without clicking any popups, this next shortcut is the fastest.

STEP 2: INSTANTLY SAVE TO FOLDER (WIN + PRTSCN)
•  First, hold down the **Windows key**.
•  Now press the **PrtScn** key once.
•  Notice your screen dims for a brief second.
•  Open up your **File Explorer** on your taskbar.
•  From the left sidebar, click on **Pictures**.
•  Double-click right on the **Screenshots** folder.
•  Your full-screen capture is saved right here.

If you need to capture a drop-down menu that disappears, you can use the built-in Snipping Tool with a delay timer.

STEP 3: USE THE SNIPPING TOOL APP WITH A TIMER
•  First, click on your **Start button**.
•  In the search bar, type **Snipping Tool**.
•  Press the **Enter** key to open it.
•  Click on the **Snip delay** clock icon.
•  From the dropdown, choose **3 seconds delay**.
•  Next, click on the **New** button.
•  Open your menu before the timer runs out.
•  Now drag your mouse to capture the menu.

Finally, if you only want to screenshot a single active window without cropping your desktop background, here is a secret shortcut.

STEP 4: CAPTURE ONLY THE ACTIVE WINDOW (ALT + PRTSCN)
•  First, click on the window you want.
•  Hold down the **Alt** key on keyboard.
•  Now press the **PrtScn** key once.
•  This copies only that window to clipboard.
•  Next, open **Paint** or an email draft.
•  Press **Ctrl** and **V** to paste.
•  Your window image appears without the desktop background.

You should now be able to capture any part of your screen in seconds!
