Want to reset network settings on your Windows 11 PC fast?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the exact step-by-step method to do it in under two minutes.
Let's jump right in!

STEP 1: RESET NETWORK SETTINGS
* First, click on your **Start button**.
* Open your **Settings** app.
* From the left menu, click on **Network & internet**.
* Scroll down and click on **Advanced network settings**.
* Under more settings, click on **Network reset**.
* Now click on the **Reset now** button.
* Click on **Yes** to confirm the reset.
* And let your PC restart to finish the process.

And that's how you can easily reset network settings in Windows 11!
