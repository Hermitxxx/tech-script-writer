METHOD 1: DELETE MULTIPLE SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS AT ONCE.
•  First, open the " Album " app from your HOME Menu.
•  Then, open the " Album " app from the bottom scroll bar.
•  Now, press the " X " on your controller to open the Manage Menu.
• Select the pictures or videos you want to delete.
•  After selecting your files, select " Delete" on the right side of the screen.
•  Select " Delete " to confirm the deletion.
•  Finally, review your Album gallery to verify that all selected captures have been cleared.
If you have hundreds of captures and want to wipe all of them at once, let's delete them through System Settings.
METHOD 2: DELETE ALL SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS AT ONCE VIA SYSTEM SETTINGS
•  First, from your HOME Menu, open " System Settings " from the bottom toolbar.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select " Data Management " .
•  On the right side, scroll down and select " Manage Screenshots and Videos " .
•  Select your storage location, choosing either " System Memory " or " microSD Card " .
•  Scroll down to the bottom and select " Delete All Screenshots and Videos " .
•  Select " Delete " to confirm the permanent removal of all captures.
•  Finally, check your Data Management screen to verify that your capture storage has been completely freed up.
