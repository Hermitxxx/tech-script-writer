If you want to turn on dark mode everywhere on Windows 11, then in this video I'm gonna show you how to enable it system-wide in just a few clicks. So, let's get right into it!

STEP 1: TURN ON SYSTEM-WIDE DARK MODE
•  First, right-click anywhere on your desktop.
•  From the menu, select **Personalize**.
•  Looking down the list, click on **Colors**.
•  Locate the setting labeled **Choose your mode**.
•  Click on the drop-down menu beside it.
•  From the list, go ahead and select **Dark**.
•  Your entire Windows system instantly turns dark.

Now, if you want your taskbar dark but prefer keeping your documents and apps bright, follow this next step.

STEP 2: CUSTOMIZE MODES FOR APPS AND WINDOWS
•  Click on that same drop-down menu again.
•  From the options, choose **Custom**.
•  Locate **Choose your default Windows mode**.
•  Select **Dark** to keep your taskbar dark.
•  Now locate **Choose your default app mode**.
•  Set your apps to **Dark** or **Light**.
•  Finally, close out of your **Settings** app.
