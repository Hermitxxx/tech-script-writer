If your Windows 11 PC has been feeling slow and sluggish, then in this video I'm gonna show you how to make it fast again with a few quick tweaks. So, let's get right into it!

STEP 1: LAUNCH CHRIS TITUS TECH UTILITY
•  First, right-click on your **Start button**.
•  From the menu, select **Terminal (Admin)**.
•  When prompted by Windows, click on **Yes**.
•  Type **irm christitus.com/win | iex** and press Enter.
•  Give the tool a few moments to download.
•  The **Chris Titus Tech** tool will now open.

Now that the utility is open, let's clear out hidden system junk and temporary cache slowing down your drives.

STEP 2: CLEAN DISK JUNK AND TEMPORARY FILES
•  First, click right on the **Tweaks** tab.
•  Under the options, check **Disk Cleanup**.
•  Next, check the box for **Delete Temporary Files**.
•  Over on the right, click **Run Tweaks**.
•  Give the tool a moment to clean.
•  Wait until the bottom bar shows **Done**.

Now that your storage is cleared, let's remove lagging window animations so your desktop feels instant.

STEP 3: OPTIMIZE VISUAL EFFECTS FOR PERFORMANCE
•  In the tweaks menu, find **Advanced Tweaks**.
•  Look for the option labeled **Set Display for Performance**.
•  Go ahead and check that box.
•  Over on the right, click **Run Tweaks**.
•  Wait for the process to complete.
•  Your Windows animations are now fully optimized.

Finally, let's unlock Windows 11's hidden power plan to run your processor at maximum speed.

STEP 4: UNLOCK THE ULTIMATE PERFORMANCE PLAN
•  Looking at the right-side panel, scroll down.
•  Under Performance Plans, locate **Enable Ultimate Performance**.
•  Click directly on **Enable Ultimate Performance**.
•  The plan is now added to your system.
•  Finally, close the tool and restart your PC.

Your Windows 11 PC should now feel brand new, snappy, and noticeably faster across all your apps!
