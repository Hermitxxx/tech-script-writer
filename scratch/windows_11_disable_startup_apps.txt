If your Windows 11 PC takes forever to boot up because of too many startup apps, then in this video I’m gonna show you a few quick ways to disable them.
So, let's get right into it!

STEP 1: TURN OFF STARTUP APPS IN SETTINGS
* First, press the windows key and " I " on your keyboard.
* Next, from the left sidebar, click on **Apps**.
* Looking down the menu, select **Startup**.
* Once inside, sort your list by **Startup impact**.
* Locate any app you don't need on boot.
* From here, switch the toggle right next to it to **OFF**.
* Repeat this for any other non-essential apps.
* And finally, close out of your **Settings** window.

Now, while Windows Settings covers most apps, some heavier background launchers only show up in Task Manager, so let's check there next.

STEP 2: DISABLE STARTUP APPS IN TASK MANAGER
* First, right-click on your **Start button**.
* From the menu, select **Task Manager**.
* Next, from the left menu, click on **Startup apps**.
* Looking down the list, review the **Startup impact** column.
* Locate any heavy app like **Spotify** or **Discord**.
* Right-click right on that app.
* From the context menu, select **Disable**.
* Repeat this for any other heavy background launchers.
* And finally, close your **Task Manager** window.

And that's pretty much all there is to managing startup apps in Windows 11!
