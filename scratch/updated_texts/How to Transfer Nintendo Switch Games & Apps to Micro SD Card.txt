STEP 1: MOVE GAMES TO MICROSD CARD IN DATA MANAGEMENT
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And move over to the gear icon for **" System Settings "** from your bottom toolbar.
* After that, scroll down over here on the left sidebar until you find **" Data Management "** .
* Once you're inside **" Data Management "**, look over here on the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Move Data Between System / microSD Card "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Select **" Move to microSD Card "** from the options over here.
* Now, browse through the list of games and apps installed on your internal memory.
* Check the box next to any game you want to move over to your memory card.
* After choosing your games, look over here on the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Move Data "** .
* Review the confirmation prompt showing the total transfer size.
* And select **" Move "** to begin the transfer.
* Wait a few moments while the progress bar copies the game data over to your microSD card.
* Once the transfer finishes, select **" OK "** .
* Finally, return to your home screen and launch your transferred game to make sure it plays smoothly.

And that's how you can quickly transfer Nintendo Switch games and apps to your microSD card.
