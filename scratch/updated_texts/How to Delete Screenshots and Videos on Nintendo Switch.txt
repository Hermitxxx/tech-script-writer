Want to delete screenshots and videos taking up space on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to delete multiple files or wipe them all at once in just a few simple steps.
Let's jump right in!

METHOD 1: DELETE MULTIPLE SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS AT ONCE
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And open up the **" Album "** app from the bottom toolbar.
* Once you're inside the album, press the " X " button on your controller to open the manage menu.
* And select **" Delete Multiple Items "** .
* Scroll through your captures and select the pictures or videos you want to remove.
* After selecting your files, move over to the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Delete Selected Items "** .
* Select **" Delete "** to confirm the deletion.
* Finally, review your album gallery to verify that all selected captures have been cleared.

If you have hundreds of captures and want to wipe all of them at once, let's delete them through System Settings.

METHOD 2: DELETE ALL SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS AT ONCE VIA SYSTEM SETTINGS
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And open up **" System Settings "** from the bottom toolbar.
* After that, scroll down over here on the left sidebar and select **" Data Management "** .
* Once you're inside **" Data Management "**, look over here on the right side.
* And select **" Manage Screenshots and Videos "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Select your storage location, choosing either **" System Memory "** or **" microSD Card "** .
* Scroll down right to the bottom over here.
* And select **" Delete All Screenshots and Videos "** .
* Select **" Delete "** to confirm the permanent removal of all captures.
* Finally, check your storage screen to verify that your capture space has been completely freed up.

And that's how you can easily delete screenshots and videos on your Nintendo Switch.
