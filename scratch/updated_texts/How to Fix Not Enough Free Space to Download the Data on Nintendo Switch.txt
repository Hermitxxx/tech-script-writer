Getting the "not enough free space to download the data" error on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
Don't worry—in this video, I'm gonna show you 3 quick solutions to free up gigabytes of storage without losing any saved data.
Let's jump right in!

SOLUTION 1: QUICK ARCHIVE UNPLAYED GAMES
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And move over to the gear icon for **" System Settings "** from your bottom toolbar.
* After that, scroll down over here on the left sidebar and select **" Data Management "** .
* Once you're inside **" Data Management "**, look over here on the right side.
* And select **" Quick Archive "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Browse through your installed titles and check the box next to any games you're not currently playing.
* Look down at the bottom of the screen to see the total storage space that will be created.
* Move over to the right side and select **" Archive Data "** .
* And select **" Archive "** to confirm.
* Don't worry, your game save data is completely safe and icons stay on your home screen for easy redownload.
* Finally, return to your home screen and check if your download starts normally.

If you want to keep all your games installed, let's move data to your microSD card.

SOLUTION 2: MOVE DATA TO YOUR MICROSD CARD
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And open up **" System Settings "** from the bottom toolbar.
* Scroll down over here on the left sidebar and select **" Data Management "** .
* Look over on the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Move Data Between System / microSD Card "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Select **" Move to microSD Card "** from the options over here.
* Choose the games or downloadable content you want to transfer.
* Select **" Move "** on the right side of the screen.
* And then select **" Move Data "** to start the transfer.
* Wait a few moments for the data to copy over to your card.
* Finally, check your system memory to verify that internal storage space has been cleared.

If you still don't have enough room, let's clear out large captured videos and screenshots.

SOLUTION 3: DELETE CAPTURED SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And open up the **" Album "** app from the bottom toolbar.
* Press the " Y " button on your controller to open the filter menu over here.
* Select **" Media Type "** .
* And choose **" Videos Only "** to find your largest capture files.
* Now, press the " X " button on your controller to open management options.
* Select **" Delete Multiple Items "** .
* Check the boxes next to all the video clips you no longer need.
* Select **" Delete Selected Items "** on the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Delete "** to confirm.
* Finally, return to your home screen and resume your download.

And that's how you can fix not enough free space to download data on your Nintendo Switch.
