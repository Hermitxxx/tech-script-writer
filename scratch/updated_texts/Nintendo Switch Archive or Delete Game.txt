Wondering whether you should archive or delete a game on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the exact difference and how to do both to free up storage space.
Let's jump right in!

METHOD 1: HOW TO ARCHIVE A GAME
* First up, go to your home screen.
* Highlight the game you want to archive.
* Press the plus button on your controller to open the options menu.
* Move down over here on the left and select **" Manage Software "** .
* Look over on the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Archive Software "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Select **" Archive "** to confirm.
* This removes the game data to free up storage space while keeping your save data completely safe.
* Notice that the game icon stays right on your home screen with a small cloud download icon.
* Finally, select the game icon anytime you want to quickly redownload it.

If you want to completely remove the game and declutter your home screen, here's how to delete it.

METHOD 2: HOW TO DELETE A GAME
* First up, highlight the game you want to delete on your home screen.
* Press the plus button on your controller to open software options.
* Scroll down over here and select **" Manage Software "** .
* Look on the right side and select **" Delete Software "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Select **" Delete "** again to confirm the deletion.
* Don't worry, your game save data is stored separately and will not be erased.
* Notice that both the game data and the game icon are now completely removed from your screen.
* Finally, check your home menu to confirm the icon is gone and your space is freed.

If you want to free up massive storage by archiving several games at once, let's use Quick Archive.

METHOD 3: BULK ARCHIVE MULTIPLE GAMES AT ONCE
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And move over to the gear icon for **" System Settings "** from your bottom toolbar.
* After that, scroll down over here on the left sidebar and select **" Data Management "** .
* Once you're inside **" Data Management "**, look over here on the right side.
* And select **" Quick Archive "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Check the box next to any games you're not currently playing.
* Look down at the bottom of the screen to see the total storage space you'll free up.
* Move over to the right side and select **" Archive Data "** .
* And select **" Archive "** to confirm.
* Finally, return to your home menu and check your newly available storage space.

And that's the difference between archiving and deleting games on your Nintendo Switch, and how to do both.
