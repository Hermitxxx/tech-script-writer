Upgrading to a bigger memory card for your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to transfer all your existing SD card data to a new SD card quickly and safely without losing anything.
Let's jump right in!

STEP 1: COPY THE NINTENDO FOLDER TO YOUR COMPUTER
* First up, press and hold the physical power button on top of your Switch for three seconds.
* Choose **" Power Options "** from the popup menu.
* And select **" Turn Off "** to shut down the console completely.
* Open up the card slot cover on your console.
* Gently press in on the microSD card until it clicks, and take it out of the slot.
* Insert the old microSD card into your computer using an SD card reader.
* Open up **" File Explorer "** on your PC.
* Click on your microSD card drive from the left sidebar over here.
* Right-click on the folder named **" Nintendo "** .
* And select **" Copy "** .
* Go over to your computer's **" Desktop "** .
* Right-click on any empty space and select **" Paste "** .
* Wait for the **" Nintendo "** folder to finish copying completely to your computer.
* Once the copy is finished, safely eject the old microSD card from your PC.

Once the files are saved on your computer, let's move them onto your new SD card.

STEP 2: TRANSFER THE NINTENDO FOLDER TO YOUR NEW SD CARD
* First up, insert your new microSD card into your computer's card reader.
* Open up **" File Explorer "** on your PC.
* Click on your new microSD card drive from the left sidebar over here.
* Make sure the card is completely empty or delete any existing files on it.
* Now, go back to your **" Desktop "** where you saved the backup folder.
* Right-click on the **" Nintendo "** folder.
* And select **" Copy "** .
* Go back to your new microSD card drive in **" File Explorer "** .
* Right-click on an empty space inside the drive.
* And select **" Paste "** .
* Wait for the entire **" Nintendo "** folder to finish copying to the new card.
* Once the transfer is complete, safely eject the new microSD card from your computer.

Now that your data is transferred, let's put the new card into your Switch and verify everything works.

STEP 3: INSERT THE NEW SD CARD AND VERIFY YOUR GAMES
* First up, slide your new microSD card firmly into the slot on your Nintendo Switch.
* Close the slot cover.
* Press the power button to turn your console back on.
* Once you're on the home screen, open up **" System Settings "** from the bottom toolbar.
* Scroll down over here on the left sidebar and select **" Data Management "** .
* Check the **" microSD Card "** section on the right to verify your new storage space.
* Finally, launch one of your installed games to confirm that all your data loads properly.

And that's how you can transfer your Nintendo Switch SD card data to a new SD card.
