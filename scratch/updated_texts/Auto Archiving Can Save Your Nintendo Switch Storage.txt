Running low on storage space on your Nintendo Switch and want the system to manage it for you?
In this video, I'll show you how auto-archiving can automatically save your storage without losing any save data.
Let's jump right in!

STEP 1: ENABLE AUTO-ARCHIVING IN DATA MANAGEMENT
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And go to the gear icon for **" System Settings "** from your bottom toolbar.
* After that, you need to scroll down over here on the left sidebar until you find **" Data Management "** .
* Once you're inside **" Data Management "**, look over here on the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Manage Software "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Browse through your library and select the specific app or game you want the console to manage.
* Once you're inside the game settings, scroll down over here.
* And select **" Prevent Auto-Archiving "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* And toggle the option over here to **" No "** to turn off the archiving prevention.
* Now, whenever your console runs low on storage during a new download, the Switch will automatically archive unplayed software to free up space.
* Don't worry, your game save data is stored separately and will never be touched.
* Finally, head back to your home screen to confirm your settings are saved.

And that's how enabling auto-archiving can save your storage space on your Nintendo Switch.
