Want to hide virtual game cards on your Nintendo Switch Lite to clean up your library?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to conceal them in just a few simple steps.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: HIDE A VIRTUAL GAME CARD
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And select **" Virtual Game Cards "** from the bottom toolbar.
* Choose your user profile linked to your Nintendo Account.
* Scroll through your library and highlight the game card you want to conceal.
* Press the plus button on your controller to open **" Options "** .
* Move down over here and select **" Hide "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* And select **" Hide "** a second time to confirm.
* Keep in mind that you can hide up to 300 virtual game cards per account.
* Finally, check your library list to confirm the virtual card is no longer visible.

And that's how you can easily hide and manage virtual game cards on your Nintendo Switch.
