Is your microSD card not working or failing to read on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you 3 simple solutions to get your memory card detected again in minutes.
Let's jump right in!

SOLUTION 1: SAFELY RE-INSERT AND RESTART YOUR CONSOLE
* First up, press and hold the physical power button on top of your Switch for three seconds.
* Choose **" Power Options "** from the popup menu.
* And select **" Turn Off "** to shut down the console completely.
* Open up the card slot cover on your console.
* Gently press in on the microSD card until it clicks, and take it out of the slot.
* Check the golden contact pins on the card for any dust or debris.
* Slide the microSD card firmly back into the slot until it clicks into place.
* Close the slot cover.
* Press the power button to turn your Nintendo Switch back on.
* Once you're back on the home screen, open up **" System Settings "** from the bottom toolbar.
* Scroll down over here on the left sidebar and select **" Data Management "** .
* And check over here on the right to verify if your **" microSD Card "** is detected.

If your Switch still doesn't recognize the card, let's check for a missing system update.

SOLUTION 2: INSTALL THE LATEST SYSTEM UPDATE
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And open up **" System Settings "** from the bottom toolbar.
* After that, scroll all the way down on the left sidebar and select **" System "** .
* Once you're inside **" System "**, look over here on the right side.
* And select **" System Update "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* If a new update is available, select **" Update "** to begin downloading.
* Wait a few moments for the console to install the update and restart automatically.
* Once the console reboots, open up **" Data Management "** in settings and check if your storage appears.

If your microSD card is still not working, let's reformat the card directly on your Switch.

SOLUTION 3: FORMAT THE MICROSD CARD ON NINTENDO SWITCH LITE
* First up, open **" System Settings "** from your home screen.
* Scroll all the way down on the left sidebar and select **" System "** .
* Once you're inside **" System "**, scroll down right to the bottom on the right side.
* And select **" Formatting Options "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Look over here and select **" Format microSD Card "** .
* Review the warning on the screen and select **" Continue "** .
* Select **" Format "** to erase the card and rebuild the file system.
* Wait a few seconds for the console to restart automatically.
* Finally, head back into **" Data Management "** to verify that your microSD card is now active and ready to use.

And that's how you can fix a microSD card not working on your Nintendo Switch.
