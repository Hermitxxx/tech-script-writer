STEP 1: PREVENT AUTO-ARCHIVING IN DATA MANAGEMENT
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And move over to the gear icon for **" System Settings "** from your bottom toolbar.
* After that, scroll down over here on the left sidebar and select **" Data Management "** .
* Once you're inside **" Data Management "**, look over here on the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Manage Software "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Browse through your library and select the specific game you want to protect from being archived.
* Once you're inside the game settings, scroll down over here.
* And select **" Prevent Auto-Archiving "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* And toggle the setting over here to **" Yes "** to keep the game permanently installed.
* Repeat this process for every individual game you don't want the console to automatically touch.
* Finally, return to your home menu and verify that your protected games stay on your console.

And that's how you can prevent auto-archiving on your Nintendo Switch.
