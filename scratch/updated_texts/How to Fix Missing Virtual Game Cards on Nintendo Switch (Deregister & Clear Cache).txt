Are your virtual game cards missing on your Nintendo Switch Lite after a recent update?
In this video, I'll show you how to deregister console conflicts, clear the cache, and restart to get them back in minutes.
Let's jump right in!

SOLUTION 1: DEREGISTER CONSOLE IN NINTENDO ESHOP
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And open up **" Nintendo eShop "** from the bottom toolbar.
* Select the user profile linked to your Nintendo Account.
* Move up to the top right corner and select your **" Profile Icon "** to open Account Information.
* Look over here on the right panel and scroll down to locate **" Primary Console "** .
* And select **" Deregister "** .
* Enter your Nintendo Account password if prompted for security.
* Select **" Deregister "** again to confirm the console status removal.
* Now, press the home button to exit back to your home screen.
* Open up **" Nintendo eShop "** once more to automatically re-sync your account and license status.
* Finally, return to your home menu and check if your virtual game cards start appearing.

If your virtual game cards are still not showing up, let's clear out corrupted temporary cache data.

SOLUTION 2: CLEAR SYSTEM CACHE FOR YOUR USER PROFILE
* First up, open **" System Settings "** from your bottom toolbar.
* Scroll all the way down on the left sidebar and select **" System "** .
* Once you're inside **" System "**, scroll down right to the bottom on the right side.
* And select **" Formatting Options "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Select **" Clear Cache "** from the options over here.
* Choose your primary user profile.
* Select **" Reset "** to confirm clearing temporary cache files and saved IDs.
* Finally, confirm on-screen that your cache data has been successfully cleared.

Now that the cache has been reset, let's perform a full system reboot to reload your game licenses.

SOLUTION 3: RESTART YOUR CONSOLE TO RE-INDEX LICENSES
* First up, press and hold the physical power button on top of your console for three seconds.
* Select **" Power Options "** from the popup menu.
* And select **" Restart "** .
* Wait a few moments for your Nintendo Switch to reboot completely.
* Make sure your console is connected to the internet after the reboot.
* Open up the **" Virtual Game Cards "** menu from your home screen.
* Finally, verify that all of your missing virtual game cards have been restored to your screen.

And that's how you can fix missing virtual game cards on your Nintendo Switch after an update.
