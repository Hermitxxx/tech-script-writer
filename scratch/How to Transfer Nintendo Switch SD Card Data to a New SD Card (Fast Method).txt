"In today's video, I'm gonna show you how to transfer your existing Nintendo Switch Lite SD card data to a new SD card quickly and safely in just a few simple steps."
So, let me guide you through the steps.
STEP 1: COPY THE NINTENDO FOLDER TO YOUR COMPUTER
•  First, press and hold the power button on top of your Switch for three seconds.
•  Choose " Power Options " from the pop up menu.
•  Select " Turn Off ".
• After that, Open the kickstand on the back of your console.
•  Gently press in on the microSD card until it clicks, then pull it out of the slot.
•  Insert the old microSD card into your computer using an SD card reader.
•  Open " File Explorer " on your PC.
•  Click on your microSD card drive from the left sidebar.
•  Right-click on the folder named " Nintendo ".
•  Select " Copy ".
•  Go to your computer's " Desktop " and paste it there by pressing Ctrl and V on your keyboard.
•  Wait for the " Nintendo " folder to finish copying completely to your computer.
•  After the process is finished , Remove the old microSD card from your computer.
Once the files are saved on your computer, let's move them onto your new SD card.
STEP 2: TRANSFER THE NINTENDO FOLDER TO YOUR NEW SD CARD
•  First, insert your new microSD card into your computer's card reader.
•  Open " File Explorer " on your PC.
•  Click on your new microSD card drive from the left sidebar.
•  Make sure the card is completely empty or delete any existing files on it.
•  Now, go back to your " Desktop " where you saved the backup.
•  Right-click on the " Nintendo " folder.
•  Select " Copy " .
•  Go back to your new microSD card drive in " File Explorer " .
•  Right-click on an empty space inside the drive.
•  Select " Paste " .
•  Wait for the entire " Nintendo " folder to finish copying to the new card.
•  Then Remove the new microSD card from your computer.
Now that your data is transferred, let's put the new card into your Switch and verify everything works.
STEP 3: INSERT THE NEW SD CARD AND VERIFY YOUR GAMES
•  First, Insert your new microSD card into your Nintendo switch.
•  Then power on your switch.
•  Open " System Settings ".
•  Scroll down on the left side and select " Data Management " .
•  Check the " microSD Card " section on the right to verify your new storage space.
•  Finally, launch one of your installed games to confirm that all your data loads properly.
And that's how you can transfer your Nintendo Switch SD card data to a new SD card.
