Is your Nintendo Switch Lite running out of internal storage because of saved screenshots and videos?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to move them directly to your microSD card and free up your system memory.
Let's jump right in!

METHOD 1: COPY ALL SCREENSHOTS TO MICROSD CARD IN SYSTEM SETTINGS
* First up, go to your home screen.
* And move over to the gear icon for **" System Settings "** from your bottom toolbar.
* After that, scroll down over here on the left sidebar until you find **" Data Management "** .
* Once you're inside **" Data Management "**, look over here on the right side of the screen.
* And select **" Manage Screenshots and Videos "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* Look under the storage options and select **" System Memory "** .
* Once you're inside here, look down at the options.
* And select **" Copy All Screenshots and Videos to microSD Card "** .
* Look at the confirmation prompt on screen.
* And select **" Copy "** to begin transferring all your captures.
* Wait a few moments while the progress bar copies the files over to your card.
* Once the transfer finishes, select **" OK "** .
* Finally, check your storage screen to verify that your captures have been copied to your card.

If you only want to transfer a single picture or video instead of copying everything, let's use the Album.

METHOD 2: COPY INDIVIDUAL SCREENSHOTS FROM THE ALBUM
* First up, return to your home screen.
* And open up the **" Album "** app from your bottom toolbar.
* Browse through your gallery and select the specific screenshot or video you want to move.
* Once the capture opens up full screen, look down in the bottom right corner.
* And select **" Sharing and Editing "** .
* Look over here on the menu options that pop up.
* And select **" Copy "** .
* When the prompt asks to copy to the microSD card, select **" Copy "** to confirm.
* Wait a second for the confirmation message to appear.
* And select **" OK "** .
* Finally, check your capture in the album to verify that it now shows the microSD card badge.

To keep your system memory clean in the future, let's set your microSD card as the default save location.

METHOD 3: SET MICROSD CARD AS DEFAULT SAVE LOCATION
* First up, open up **" System Settings "** from your home screen.
* Scroll down over here on the left sidebar and select **" Data Management "** .
* On the right side, select **" Manage Screenshots and Videos "** .
* Look right at the top of the screen where it says **" Save Location "** .
* Let's tap on it.
* And select **" microSD Card "** from the options over here.
* Now, all future screenshots and video clips you capture will save directly to your memory card automatically.
* Finally, return to your home screen to confirm your settings are active.

And that's how you can easily move screenshots to your microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
