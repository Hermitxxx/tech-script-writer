Want to clear annoying typos or wipe custom predictive text suggestions from your Nintendo Switch keyboard?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to reset your keyboard dictionary back to factory defaults in seconds.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: RESET KEYBOARD DICTIONARY IN SYSTEM SETTINGS
* First go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** on the bottom toolbar for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
* After entering into **System**, scroll down to the very bottom and choose **Formatting Options**.
* Enter your PIN if you have **Parental Controls** turned on.
* Once inside **Formatting Options**, click on **Reset Keyboard**.
* Pick the specific user profile whose keyboard history you want to clear.
* Review the on-screen prompt and hit **Reset**.
* Keep in mind that this only erases custom learned words and will not delete any game save data.
* Click on **OK** once the confirmation notice appears.
* Check that your predictive text dictionary is reset to factory defaults.

If you have multiple user profiles on your console, or want to verify that your predictive suggestions are wiped, let's open up a quick text field to test your fresh keyboard.

STEP 2: TEST YOUR KEYBOARD IN THE USER NICKNAME MENU
* Press the physical **Home button** on your controller to return to your home screen.
* Move up to the top left corner and select your **User Profile icon**.
* Once inside your user page, head down and click on **User Settings**.
* Choose **Nickname** to open up the on-screen virtual keyboard.
* Type a few test letters into the input box.
* Verify that your old personalized word predictions and typos no longer appear above the keys.
* Press the physical **Home button** once more to return to your home screen.

And that's how you can easily reset your keyboard settings on your Nintendo Switch.
