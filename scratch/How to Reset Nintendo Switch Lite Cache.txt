Is your Nintendo Switch Lite running slow or running into weird Nintendo eShop and browser glitches?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to quickly reset your cache to get everything running smoothly again.
Let's jump right in!

STEP 1: RESET USER AND BROWSER CACHE IN FORMATTING OPTIONS
* First go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and select **System**.
* After entering into **System**, scroll down to the very bottom and choose **Formatting Options**.
* Once inside **Formatting Options**, click on **Reset Cache**.
* Pick the user profile you want to clear.
* Review the on-screen prompt and hit **Reset**.
* Keep in mind that your downloaded games and save data will not be deleted.
* Click on **OK** once the confirmation message appears.
* Check that your user cache is now completely refreshed.

If clearing your profile cache didn't completely resolve the lagging or crashing, temporary system memory cache might still be stuck in RAM, so let's perform a full power cycle to flush it out.

STEP 2: PERFORM A FULL POWER CYCLE TO CLEAR SYSTEM RAM CACHE
* Locate the physical **POWER Button** on the top left edge of your console.
* Press and hold down the **POWER Button** for about three seconds.
* Choose **Power Options** from the popup menu.
* Click on **Restart**.
* If your console is completely frozen, hold down the physical **POWER Button** for twelve seconds to force a hardware shutdown.
* Press the **POWER Button** once again to turn the console back on.
* Wait a few moments for the Nintendo logo to appear and load your home menu.
* Verify that your system memory cache is cleared and your games run smoothly.

And that's how you can easily reset your cache on your Nintendo Switch Lite.
