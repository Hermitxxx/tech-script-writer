Did your Nintendo Switch break, get lost, or get stolen while it was still set as your primary console?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to remotely deregister it from any web browser and set up your new Switch in seconds.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: DEREGISTER BROKEN SWITCH FROM NINTENDO ACCOUNT
* Open any web browser on your phone or computer.
* In the address bar, type " accounts.nintendo.com " and hit enter.
* Log into your account using your email address and password.
* From the left sidebar, click on **Shop Menu**.
* After entering into **Shop Menu**, scroll down to the bottom and select **Deregister Primary Console**.
* Enter your Nintendo Account password again to verify your identity.
* Review the on-screen prompt and choose **Deregister**.
* Check that your screen confirms your previous console has been deregistered.
* Keep in mind that Nintendo allows remote website deregistration once every twelve months.

Once your old console has been unlinked through the website, let's head over to your replacement Nintendo Switch to make it your active primary system.

STEP 2: REGISTER YOUR REPLACEMENT SWITCH AS PRIMARY
* First, turn on your new or replacement Nintendo Switch.
* Make sure you are signed into the user profile linked to your Nintendo Account.
* From your home screen, move over to the orange bag icon for **Nintendo eShop**.
* Once the store loads, head up to your **User Profile icon** in the top-right corner.
* Scroll down the account information screen to the **Primary Console** section.
* Check that your console status now displays that it is registered as your primary console.
* Press the physical **Home button** on your controller to return to your home screen.

And that's how you can remotely deregister a broken or lost primary Switch and set up your new console.
Keep in mind Nintendo allows website deregistration once per year, so if your account reaches that limit, you'll need to contact official Nintendo support for assistance.
