Are you getting the frustrating error code 2101-0001 on your Nintendo Switch and can't use your console?
In this video, I'm gonna walk you through the top fixes to troubleshoot and resolve this error in minutes.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: PERFORM A FULL RESTART ON YOUR CONSOLE
* First, press and hold down the physical **POWER Button** on the top left edge of your console for at least three seconds.
* From the power menu that pops up on screen, select **Power Options**.
* Now choose **Restart**.
* If your screen is completely frozen on the error display, hold down the physical **POWER Button** for a full twelve seconds to force a hard shutdown, then press it once to power it back on.
* Verify that your Switch reboots cleanly to the home screen without the error code returning.

If restarting your Switch didn't resolve the issue and the error pops back up, let's check for any missing system updates that might contain an official stability patch.

STEP 2: CHECK FOR A SYSTEM UPDATE
* From your home screen, navigate over to the **gear icon** on the bottom toolbar for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down the left sidebar and click on **System**.
* Move over to the right side of the screen.
* Hit **System Update** at the very top of the list.
* The console will automatically check for any pending software updates.
* If an update is available, choose **Update** to begin downloading and installing the new firmware.
* Wait for the console to install the update and reboot, then check that your system version displays **Current**.

If updating your console still didn't clear the error, a corrupted memory card might be causing a power conflict, so let's remove your microSD card next.

STEP 3: REMOVE THE MICROSD CARD
* Press and hold the physical **POWER Button** on top for three seconds.
* Click on **Power Options** and select **Turn Off**.
* Flip your Nintendo Switch around to the back and gently lift open the kickstand.
* Press the **microSD Card** inward until you hear a soft click, then slide it out of the slot.
* Inspect the card pins for any dirt or damage, and leave the card out of the console for now.
* Press the **POWER Button** on top to turn your console back on.
* Confirm that your console boots smoothly into your home screen without triggering the error code.

If the error code is still persisting with the card removed, the problem could be tied to power delivery, so let's test a direct connection with a different charging adapter or cable.

STEP 4: TEST A DIFFERENT CHARGING ADAPTER OR CABLE
* Unplug your Switch from the dock and disconnect any attached charging cables.
* Take a close look at the **USB-C port** on the bottom of the console to make sure there is no lint, debris, or bent pins.
* Grab an official **Nintendo Switch AC Adapter** or a known working USB-C cable and plug it directly into a wall outlet rather than a power strip or hub.
* Connect the cable straight into the bottom charging port of your console.
* Let your Switch sit and charge undisturbed for at least twenty minutes.
* Check that the green battery charging icon displays in the top corner and verify that the error code is gone.

If none of these software and charging steps resolved the problem, your console is dealing with an internal component failure.

STEP 5: IDENTIFY HARDWARE FAILURE AND CONTACT NINTENDO REPAIR
* If the error code still persists after trying all these troubleshooting steps, your console is experiencing an internal hardware fault.
* Error code 2101-0001 usually points to a damaged USB-C charging circuit or a blown internal power management chip.
* On your phone or computer, open your web browser and head over to the official **Nintendo Support** website.
* Navigate over to the **Consumer Service** section and click on **Set Up a Repair**.
* Follow the on-screen steps to register your serial number and submit an official repair ticket with Nintendo technicians.

And that's how you can troubleshoot and fix error code 2101-0001 on your Nintendo Switch.
If none of these solutions work on your device, it's most likely an internal hardware issue, so you'll need to contact official customer support to set up a repair.
