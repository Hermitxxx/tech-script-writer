Want to change your DNS to 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8 in Windows 11 fast?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the exact step-by-step method to set it up in under two minutes.
Let's jump right in!

STEP 1: ACCESS MANUAL DNS SETTINGS
* First, press the windows key and " I " on your keyboard.
* Next, from the left menu, click on **Network & internet**.
* Select your active connection, either **Ethernet** or **Wi-Fi**.
* Looking down the page, locate **DNS server assignment**.
* Click right on the **Edit** button next to it.
* In the popup, click the drop-down menu.
* Go ahead and change it to **Manual**.
* Now, toggle the **IPv4** switch to **ON**.

Now that IPv4 settings are open, you can enter either Cloudflare or Google DNS depending on your preference.

STEP 2: CONFIGURE CLOUDFLARE OR GOOGLE DNS
* For Cloudflare, type " 1.1.1.1 " into **Preferred DNS**.
* Next, type " 1.0.0.1 " into **Alternate DNS**.
* If you prefer Google, type " 8.8.8.8 " into **Preferred DNS**.
* And type " 8.8.4.4 " into **Alternate DNS**.
* Once your numbers are entered, click on **Save**.
* And finally, check that your new DNS appears.

And that's pretty much all there is to configuring custom DNS in Windows 11!
