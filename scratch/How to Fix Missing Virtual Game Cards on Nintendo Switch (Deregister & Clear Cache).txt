Are your virtual game cards missing on your Nintendo Switch Lite after a recent update?
In this video, I'll show you how to deregister console conflicts, clear the cache, and restart to get them back in just a few simple steps!
SOLUTION 1: DEREGISTER CONSOLE IN NINTENDO ESHOP
•  First, from your HOME Menu, open " Nintendo eShop " from the bottom toolbar.
•  Select the user profile linked to your Nintendo Account.
•  Select your " Profile Icon " in the top-right corner to open Account Information.
•  Go to " Settings " from the menu on the left.
•  Scroll down the right panel to locate the " Pass-Enabled Console " section.
•  Select " Deregister " .
•  Enter your Nintendo Account password if prompted for security.
•  Select " Deregister " again to confirm the console status removal.
•  Now, press the " HOME Button " to exit back to your home screen.
•  Open the " Nintendo eShop " once more to automatically re-sync your account and license status.
•  Finally, return to your HOME Menu and check if your virtual game cards start appearing.
If your virtual game cards are still not showing up, let's clear out corrupted temporary cache data.
Sometimes this might be the reason for the virtual cards not appearing on your Nintendo switch lite.
SOLUTION 2: CLEAR SYSTEM CACHE FOR YOUR USER PROFILE
•  First, from your HOME Menu, open " System Settings " from the bottom toolbar.
•  Scroll all the way down on the left sidebar and select " System " .
•  Scroll to the very bottom on the right side and select " Formatting Options " .
•  Select " Reset Cache ".
•  Select " Reset " to confirm clearing temporary cache files and saved IDs.
•  This will reset the cache data from your Nintendo switch lite.
Now that the cache has been reset, let's perform a full system reboot to reload your game licenses.
SOLUTION 3: RESTART YOUR CONSOLE TO RE-INDEX LICENSES
•  Press and hold the physical " POWER Button " on the top of your console for three seconds.
•  Select " Power Options " from the menu.
•  Select " Restart " .
•  Wait a few moments for your Nintendo Switch to reboot completely.
•  Ensure your console is connected to the internet after a successful reboot.
•  Open the " Virtual Game Cards " menu.
•  Finally, you will see that all of your missing virtual game cards have been restored to your screen.
And that's how you can fix missing virtual game cards on your Nintendo Switch after an update.
