Want to stop your Nintendo Switch from making chime sounds every time you get a notification?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to turn off your notification sounds in seconds.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: TURN OFF NOTIFICATION SOUND IN SYSTEM SETTINGS
* First, go to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** on the bottom toolbar for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Notifications**.
* Move over to the right side of the screen.
* Scroll down to find the **Notification Sound** option.
* Switch the toggle to **OFF** to silence all notification chimes.
* Check that the setting now displays **OFF**.
* Press the physical **Home button** on your controller to return to your home screen.

And that's how you can easily turn off notification sounds on your Nintendo Switch.
