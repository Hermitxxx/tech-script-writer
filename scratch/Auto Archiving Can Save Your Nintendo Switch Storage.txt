Running low on storage space on your Nintendo Switch and want the system to manage it for you?
In this video, I'll show you how auto-archiving can automatically save your storage without losing any save data.
Let's jump right in!
•  First, go to your HOME Menu.
•  Move over to the gear icon for " System Settings ".
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select " Data Management " .
•  On the right side of the screen, select " Manage Software " .
•  Browse through your library and select the specific app or game you want the console to manage.
•  Select " Prevent Auto-Archiving " .
•  Toggle the option to " No " to turn off the archiving prevention.
•  Now, whenever your console runs low on storage, the Switch can automatically archive unplayed software to free up space.
•  Don't worry, your game save data is stored separately and will never be deleted.
And that's how enabling auto-archiving can save your storage space on your Nintendo Switch.
