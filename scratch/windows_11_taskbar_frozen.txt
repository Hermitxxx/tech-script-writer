Is your Windows 11 taskbar suddenly frozen and won't respond to your clicks?
Don't worry—in this video I'll show you a few quick step-by-step fixes, so let's get right into it!

STEP 1: RESTART WINDOWS EXPLORER IN TASK MANAGER
* First, right-click on your **Start button**.
* From the menu, select **Task Manager**.
* Once inside, ensure you're on the **Processes** tab.
* Looking down the list, locate **Windows Explorer**.
* Right-click directly on **Windows Explorer**.
* From the context menu, select **Restart**.
* Let's give your screen a moment to refresh.
* And finally, test if your taskbar clicks work.

Now, if restarting Windows Explorer didn't unfreeze your taskbar, corrupted system files might be causing the glitch, so let's run a system scan to fix them.

STEP 2: RUN SYSTEM FILE CHECKER SCAN
* First, right-click on your **Start button**.
* From the menu, select **Terminal (Admin)**.
* When prompted by User Account Control, click **Yes**.
* Now, type " sfc /scannow " into the console.
* Press Enter on your keyboard to run it.
* Let's give the scan a few minutes to complete.
* Once finished, restart your PC to apply repairs.
* And check if your taskbar responds after rebooting.

If your taskbar is still unresponsive or frozen, restarting the Windows input loader service will usually solve the problem immediately.

STEP 3: RESTART THE WINDOWS CTF LOADER
* First, press the windows key and " R " on your keyboard.
* In the Run box, type " c:\windows\system32\ctfmon.exe ".
* Go ahead and click on **OK**.
* Let's wait a couple of seconds for it to start.
* And finally, test your taskbar to ensure it works smoothly.

And that's how you can easily fix a frozen taskbar in Windows 11!
