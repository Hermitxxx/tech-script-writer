In today's video, I'm going to show you how to fix a microSD card that's not working on your Nintendo Switch Lite in just a few simple steps.
So, let me guide through it.
SOLUTION 1: SAFELY RE-INSERT AND RESTART YOUR CONSOLE
•  First, press and hold the power button on top of your Switch for three seconds.
•  Choose " Power Options " from the popup menu.
•  Select " Turn Off " .
•  Open the kickstand on the back of your console.
•  Gently take out the microSD card from your Switch.
•  Check the golden contact pins on the card for any dust or debris.
•  Slide the microSD card firmly back into the slot.
•  Close the kickstand.
•  Press the power button to turn your Nintendo Switch back on.
•  Finally, open " System Settings ".
•  Scroll down and go to " Data Management " and check if your microSD card is detected or not.
If your Switch still doesn't recognize the card, let's check for a missing system update.
SOLUTION 2: INSTALL THE LATEST SYSTEM UPDATE
•  First, go to your home screen.
•  Select " System Settings " from the bottom toolbar.
•  Scroll all the way down and select " System ".
•  Select " System Update " on the right side.
•  If a new update is available, select " Update " to begin downloading.
•  Wait for the console to install the update and restart.
•  After the update, open " Data Management " and check if your storage appears.
If your microSD card is still not working, let's reformat the card directly on your Switch.
SOLUTION 3: FORMAT THE MICROSD CARD ON NINTENDO SWITCH LITE
•  First, open " System Settings " from your home screen.
•  Scroll down and select " System ".
•  Scroll down to the bottom of the right side and select " Formatting Options " .
•  Select " Format microSD Card " .
•  Review the warning on the screen and select " Continue " .
•  Select " Format " to erase the card and rebuild the file system.
•  Wait a few seconds for the console to restart automatically.
•  Finally, head back into " Data Management " to verify that your microSD card is now active and ready to use.
And that's how you can fix a microSD card not working on your Nintendo Switch.
