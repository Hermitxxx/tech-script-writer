Want to prevent your Nintendo Switch Lite from automatically archiving your favorite games?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to disable auto-archiving in just a few quick taps.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap on **Manage Software**.
* Choose the specific game you want to protect.
* Inside game settings, scroll down and select **Prevent Auto-Archiving**.
* Go ahead and switch this toggle to **Yes**.
* Repeat this for any games you want to keep.
* Now head back to your home menu.
* And verify your protected games stay installed.

And that's how you can prevent auto-archiving on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
