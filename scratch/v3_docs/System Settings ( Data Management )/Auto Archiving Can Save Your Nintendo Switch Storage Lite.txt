Want to save gigabytes of storage on your Nintendo Switch Lite without losing your save data?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how auto-archiving frees up storage automatically in just a few taps.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap on **Manage Software**.
* Choose the specific game you want to manage.
* Inside the game options, select **Prevent Auto-Archiving**.
* Go ahead and set this option to **No**.
* This lets your Switch archive unplayed games automatically.
* Keep in mind your save data stays completely safe.
* And check that your storage is now managed automatically.

And that's how enabling auto-archiving saves storage on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
