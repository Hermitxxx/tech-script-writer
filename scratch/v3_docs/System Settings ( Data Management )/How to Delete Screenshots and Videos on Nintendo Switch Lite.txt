Want to delete unwanted screenshots and video clips from your Nintendo Switch Lite to free up space?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the quickest ways to delete multiple captures or wipe them all at once.
Let's get right into it!

METHOD 1: DELETE MULTIPLE SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS FROM ALBUM
* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the blue **Album** icon.
* Inside the album, press the " X " button.
* Scroll through and check the captures you want to remove.
* Move over to the right and select **Delete**.
* Confirm by choosing **Delete** once more.
* And check your album to verify the captures are gone.

Now, if you have hundreds of captures and want to clear out everything at once, let's wipe them all directly from your system settings.

METHOD 2: DELETE ALL SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS IN SETTINGS
* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, select **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* Choose either **System Memory** or **microSD Card**.
* Scroll down and select **Delete All Screenshots and Videos**.
* Confirm by choosing **Delete Screenshots and Videos**.
* And check your storage to verify all space is freed up.

And that's how you can easily delete screenshots and videos on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
