Want to quickly transfer your Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots directly to your iPhone?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to send your captures wirelessly in just a few simple taps.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: CHOOSE SCREENSHOTS ON NINTENDO SWITCH
* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Open the **Album** from the bottom toolbar.
* Open the screenshot you want to send.
* Press the " A " button on your console.
* From the menu, select **Send to Smartphone**.
* And leave the first **QR code** open on your screen.

Now grab your iPhone so we can connect to your Switch and download your photos.

STEP 2: SCAN QR CODES ON IPHONE
* Open up the **Camera** app on your iPhone.
* Point your camera at the first **QR code**.
* Tap the network prompt and hit **Connect**.
* Now point your camera at the second **QR code**.
* Tap the website link that appears on screen.
* In Safari, tap the blue **Download** button.
* Check that your screenshot appears in **Photos**.
* And on your Switch, select **End** to disconnect.

And that's how you can easily copy Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots to your iPhone!
