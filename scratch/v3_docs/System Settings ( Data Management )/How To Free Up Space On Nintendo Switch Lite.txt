Running out of storage space on your Nintendo Switch Lite and need room for more games?
In this video, I'm gonna walk you through the three best methods to free up space fast.
Let's get right into it!

METHOD 1: QUICK ARCHIVE UNPLAYED GAMES
* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap on **Quick Archive**.
* Check the boxes next to games you aren't playing.
* Move over to the right and select **Archive Data**.
* Confirm by choosing **Archive**.
* Keep in mind your save data remains completely safe.
* Now return to your home screen.
* And check that your storage space is freed up.

Now, if you want to keep your games installed, let's move them directly to your microSD card.

METHOD 2: MOVE GAMES TO MICROSD CARD
* Open up **System Settings** from your home screen.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap **Move Data Between System / microSD Card**.
* Choose **Move to microSD Card** from the options.
* Select the games or DLC you want to move.
* Move over to the right and click **Move Data**.
* Confirm by choosing **Move**.
* Give it a few moments to copy files over.
* And check your storage to verify internal memory is freed.

If you still need extra gigabytes, let's clear out bulky captured videos and screenshots.

METHOD 3: DELETE CAPTURED VIDEOS AND SCREENSHOTS
* Head back to your home screen.
* Move over to the blue **Album** icon.
* Filter by **Videos Only** to find your largest files.
* Press the " X " button to open manage options.
* Check all the video clips you no longer need.
* Move over to the right side and click **Delete**.
* Confirm by choosing **Delete** once more.
* Now return to your home screen.
* And check that your available storage space has increased.

And that's how you can easily free up storage on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
