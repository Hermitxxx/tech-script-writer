Want to copy all your screenshots and gameplay clips from your SD card over to system memory?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to transfer all your captures in just a few clicks.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, select **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* Select **microSD Card** from the menu.
* Click on **Copy All Screenshots and Videos to System Memory**.
* Give the progress bar a moment to copy your files.
* Select **OK** once the transfer finishes.
* And check your storage to confirm your captures are copied.

And that's how you can copy all SD card screenshots and videos to system memory on Nintendo Switch Lite!
