Need to redownload archived or deleted games on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to get them back in just a few simple taps.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **Virtual Game Cards** icon.
* Select the user profile that purchased the game.
* Scroll through your library and find your title.
* Click on the game card to open details.
* Choose **Load on this system** to start downloading.
* Give it a moment to authenticate your license.
* Now return to your home screen.
* And check that your game is installing.

And that's how you can redownload archived or deleted games on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
