Want to free up internal storage on your Nintendo Switch Lite by moving all your game captures to an SD card?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to copy all your screenshots and videos to a microSD card in seconds.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, select **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* From here, choose **System Memory**.
* Select **Copy All Screenshots and Videos to microSD Card**.
* Give the progress bar a moment to copy your files.
* Tap on **OK** once the transfer finishes.
* And check your storage to confirm your captures are moved.

And that's how you can copy all your screenshots and videos to a microSD card on Nintendo Switch Lite!
