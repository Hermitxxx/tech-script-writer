Want to transfer your screenshots from system memory to an SD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to move all your captures in just a few simple taps.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, select **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* Under storage options, choose **System Memory**.
* Click on **Copy All Screenshots and Videos to microSD Card**.
* Give the progress bar a moment to copy your files.
* Tap on **OK** once the transfer finishes.
* And check your storage to confirm your captures are copied.

And that's how you can easily move screenshots to your microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
