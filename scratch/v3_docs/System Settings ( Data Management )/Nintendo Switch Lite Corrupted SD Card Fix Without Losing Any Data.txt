Getting a corrupted microSD card error on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
Don't panic—in this video, I'm gonna show you two proven solutions to fix it without losing any of your saved data.
Let's get right into it!

SOLUTION 1: REPAIR MICROSD CARD USING WINDOWS
* First, turn off your Nintendo Switch completely.
* Open the card slot cover and take out your microSD card.
* Insert the microSD card into your computer's card reader.
* Press the windows key on your keyboard.
* In the search bar, type " cmd ".
* Right-click on **Command Prompt** and choose **Run as administrator**.
* Click **Yes** if the permission prompt appears.
* In the command prompt, type " chkdsk E: /f /r ".
* Make sure you replace " E: " with your card's drive letter.
* Press Enter on your keyboard to run the scan.
* Wait for Windows to repair any corrupted sectors.
* Safely eject the card from your computer.
* Slide the microSD card back into your Switch and power it on.
* And check if your games and data now load smoothly.

Now, if Windows fails to repair the card, let's manually back up your files and reformat the card directly on your Switch.

SOLUTION 2: MANUALLY BACK UP AND REFORMAT SD CARD
* Turn off your Switch and take out your microSD card.
* Connect the card to your computer using your reader.
* Open up **File Explorer** on your PC.
* Click on your microSD card drive on the left.
* Right-click on the folder named **Nintendo**.
* Select **Copy**.
* Head over to your computer's **Desktop**.
* Right-click on an empty space and choose **Paste**.
* Once the copy finishes, safely eject the card from your PC.
* Slide the microSD card into your Switch and turn it on.
* Open **System Settings** and select **System**.
* Scroll to the bottom and select **Formatting Options**.
* From here, choose **Format microSD Card**.
* Select **Continue** and confirm to format the card.
* Give your Switch a moment to format and restart.
* Turn off your console and plug the card back into your PC.
* Copy the backed-up **Nintendo** folder from your desktop.
* Paste it directly onto the root of your fresh microSD card.
* Safely eject the card from your computer.
* Insert the card back into your Nintendo Switch and power it on.
* And verify that all your games and save data load properly.

And that's how you can fix a corrupted microSD card on your Nintendo Switch without losing your data!
