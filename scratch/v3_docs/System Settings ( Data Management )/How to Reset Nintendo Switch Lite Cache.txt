Is your Nintendo Switch Lite running slow or running into weird eShop and browser glitches?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to quickly reset your cache to smooth things out.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down and select **System**.
* Scroll to the bottom and select **Formatting Options**.
* From here, click on **Reset Cache**.
* Review the prompt and go ahead and hit **Reset**.
* Keep in mind your games and save data stay safe.
* Tap on **OK** once the confirmation appears.
* And check that your console is running smoothly.

And that's how you can easily reset your cache on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
