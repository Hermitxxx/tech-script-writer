Want to transfer your Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots and gameplay videos over to your PC?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the quickest ways to copy your clips using a USB cable or microSD card.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: TRANSFER VIA USB CABLE
* From your home screen, open **System Settings**.
* Scroll down and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, select **Manage Screenshots and Videos**.
* Select **Copy to a Computer via USB Connection**.
* Plug a USB-C cable into your Switch and PC.
* Check your Switch screen until it says **Connected to the computer**.
* Now open **File Explorer** on your PC.
* Click on **This PC** on the left.
* Double-click on **Nintendo Switch** under your drives.
* Open the **Album** folder.
* Browse into your specific game folder.
* Select the clips or screenshots you want.
* Copy and paste them onto your computer.
* Look back at your Switch and select **Disconnect**.
* And safely unplug the cable from your console.

Now, if your PC doesn't recognize the USB connection, you can also transfer files using your microSD card.

STEP 2: TRANSFER FILES USING MICROSD CARD
* Hold down the **POWER Button** for three seconds.
* Choose **Power Options** from the popup menu.
* Select **Turn Off** to shut down completely.
* Gently take out your **microSD card**.
* Insert the card into your computer's reader.
* Open **File Explorer** on your PC.
* Click on your **microSD card** drive on the left.
* Open the **Nintendo** folder.
* Open the **Album** folder.
* Browse into your game folder.
* Copy and paste your desired clips to your PC.
* Safely eject the card from your computer.
* And slide the card back into your Switch.

And that's how you can easily copy screenshots and videos from your Nintendo Switch Lite to your PC!
