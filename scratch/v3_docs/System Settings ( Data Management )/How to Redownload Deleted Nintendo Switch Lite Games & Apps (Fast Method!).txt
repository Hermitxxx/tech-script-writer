Looking for the fastest way to redownload deleted games and apps on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna walk you through the easiest method to get them right back.
Let's get right into it!

* First, make sure your Switch is connected to Wi-Fi.
* From your home screen, open **Virtual Game Cards**.
* Select the user profile linked to your account.
* Highlight the deleted game or app in your library.
* Click on the game card to open its options.
* Choose **Load on this system** from the menu.
* Give it a moment while your console authenticates.
* Now return to your home menu.
* And check that your game is downloaded and ready.
