Did you just run out of internal storage on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the fastest way to transfer your games and apps directly to your microSD card.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap **Move Data Between System / microSD Card**.
* Choose **Move to microSD Card** from the options.
* Check the games or apps you want to move.
* Move over to the right and click **Move Data**.
* Review the transfer size prompt.
* Confirm by choosing **Move**.
* Give the progress bar a moment to copy files.
* Hit **OK** once the transfer finishes.
* Now launch your game to verify it plays smoothly.

And that's how you can easily transfer games and apps to your microSD card on Nintendo Switch Lite!
