Are your Virtual Game Cards missing or not showing up on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to restore all your missing game cards in just a few minutes.
Let's get right into it!

SOLUTION 1: DEREGISTER CONSOLE IN NINTENDO ESHOP
* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the orange **Nintendo eShop** icon.
* Select the user profile linked to your account.
* Tap on your **Profile Icon** in the top-right corner.
* Scroll down the left menu and select **Settings**.
* On the right panel, look for **Primary Console**.
* Go ahead and click on **Deregister**.
* Enter your Nintendo Account password if prompted.
* Confirm by selecting **Deregister** again.
* Press the **Home button** to return to your home screen.
* Reopen **Nintendo eShop** to re-sync your licenses.
* And check if your virtual cards pop back up.

Now, if your virtual game cards are still not showing up, let's clear out any corrupted system cache files.

SOLUTION 2: CLEAR SYSTEM CACHE
* From your home screen, open **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down and select **System**.
* Scroll to the bottom and tap on **Formatting Options**.
* From here, choose **Reset Cache**.
* Confirm by choosing **Reset** to clear the cache.
* And verify that the cache reset confirmation appears.

Now that your cache is cleared, let's perform a quick reboot so your console can rebuild its license index.

SOLUTION 3: RESTART YOUR CONSOLE
* Press and hold the **POWER Button** for three seconds.
* Choose **Power Options** from the popup menu.
* Go ahead and tap on **Restart**.
* Give your console a moment to reboot.
* Make sure your Switch reconnects to Wi-Fi.
* Open the **Virtual Game Cards** menu.
* And verify that all your cards are now restored.

And that's how you can easily fix missing virtual game cards on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
