Want to hide virtual game cards on your Nintendo Switch Lite to declutter your library?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to conceal them in just a few simple taps.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **Virtual Game Cards** option.
* Select the user profile linked to your account.
* Choose the specific game card you want to conceal.
* On the right popup menu, click on **Options**.
* Scroll down and select **Hide**.
* Confirm by choosing **Hide** a second time.
* And check your library to verify the card is hidden.

And that's how you can easily hide virtual game cards on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
