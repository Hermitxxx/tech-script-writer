Is your microSD card not recognized or working properly on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna walk you through the top fixes to get your memory card detected in minutes.
Let's get right into it!

SOLUTION 1: SAFELY RE-INSERT AND RESTART CONSOLE
* First, press and hold the **POWER Button** for three seconds.
* Choose **Power Options** from the popup menu.
* Select **Turn Off** to shut down completely.
* Open the card slot cover on the bottom.
* Gently take out your microSD card.
* Inspect the gold pins for dust or dirt.
* Slide the microSD card firmly back into place.
* Press the **POWER Button** to turn your console back **ON**.
* Now head into **System Settings**.
* Scroll down and select **Data Management**.
* And check if your **microSD Card** is now recognized.

Now, if your console still doesn't detect the card, you might be missing a required system update, so let's check for that next.

SOLUTION 2: INSTALL LATEST SYSTEM UPDATE
* Open up **System Settings** from your home screen.
* Scroll all the way down and select **System**.
* Tap on **System Update** at the very top.
* If an update appears, select **Update**.
* Give your console a moment to restart.
* Now head back into **Data Management**.
* And verify if your storage card shows up.

Still having trouble? That usually means the card's file format is glitched, so in this final step, let's reformat the card directly on your Switch.

SOLUTION 3: FORMAT MICROSD CARD
* From your home screen, open **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down and select **System**.
* Scroll to the bottom and select **Formatting Options**.
* From here, choose **Format microSD Card**.
* Review the warning and select **Continue**.
* Confirm by choosing **Format** to erase the card.
* Give your console a moment to restart automatically.
* Now head into **Data Management**.
* And verify that your microSD card is active and ready.

And that's how you can fix a microSD card not working on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
