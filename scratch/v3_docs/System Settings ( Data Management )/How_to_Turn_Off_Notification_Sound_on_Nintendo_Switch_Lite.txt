Want to stop your Nintendo Switch Lite from making chime sounds every time you get a notification?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to turn off notification sounds in seconds.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Notifications**.
* On the right menu, look for **Notification Sound**.
* Go ahead and switch this setting to **Off**.
* And check that the setting now displays **Off**.

And that's how you can easily turn off notification sounds on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
