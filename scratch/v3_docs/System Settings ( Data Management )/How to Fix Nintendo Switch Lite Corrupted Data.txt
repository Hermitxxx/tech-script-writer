Is your Nintendo Switch Lite showing a corrupted data error when you try to launch a game?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to scan and fix corrupted data in just a few simple steps.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap on **Manage Software**.
* Choose the specific game showing the corrupted error.
* Scroll to the bottom and click **Check for Corrupted Data**.
* Give your console a few moments to scan the files.
* If corruption is found, go ahead and select **Redownload**.
* This replaces damaged files without touching your save data.
* If no corruption was found, tap **OK** to finish.
* Now launch your game.
* And verify that it opens smoothly without any errors.

And that's pretty much all there is to fixing corrupted data on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
