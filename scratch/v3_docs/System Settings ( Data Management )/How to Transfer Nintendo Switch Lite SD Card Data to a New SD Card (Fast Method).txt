Upgrading to a bigger microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite and want to transfer all your data?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to safely transfer everything to your new card without losing anything.
Let's get right into it!

* First, press and hold the **POWER Button** for three seconds.
* Choose **Power Options** from the popup menu.
* Select **Turn Off** to shut down completely.
* Open the card slot cover on your console.
* Gently remove your old microSD card.
* Insert your old card into your computer's reader.
* Open up **File Explorer** on your PC.
* Click on your microSD card drive on the left.
* Right-click on the folder named **Nintendo**.
* Select **Copy**.
* Head over to your computer's **Desktop**.
* Right-click on an empty space and choose **Paste**.
* Let the **Nintendo** folder finish copying completely.
* Safely eject your old microSD card from your PC.
* Now insert your new microSD card into your reader.
* Open up **File Explorer** and click your new card.
* Head back to the backup folder on your **Desktop**.
* Right-click on the **Nintendo** folder.
* Select **Copy**.
* Go back to your new microSD card in **File Explorer**.
* Right-click in the drive and choose **Paste**.
* Wait for the folder to finish copying over.
* Safely eject your new card from your computer.
* Slide the new card firmly into your Nintendo Switch.
* Press the **POWER Button** to turn your console on.
* Open **System Settings** and select **Data Management**.
* Check the **microSD Card** section to verify your space.
* And launch a game to confirm everything loads properly.

And that's how you can transfer all your Nintendo Switch Lite SD card data to a new card!
