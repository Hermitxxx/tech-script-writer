Want to quickly transfer your Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots directly to your Android phone?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to send them wirelessly in seconds using QR codes.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: CHOOSE SCREENSHOTS ON NINTENDO SWITCH
* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the blue **Album** icon.
* Open the screenshot you want to transfer.
* Press the " A " button on your console.
* Click on **Send to Smartphone**.
* And leave the first **QR code** open on screen.

Now grab your Android phone so we can connect to your Switch and download your photos.

STEP 2: SCAN QR CODES ON ANDROID
* Open the **Camera** app on your phone.
* Point your camera at the first **QR code**.
* Tap on the network prompt and hit **Connect**.
* Now point your camera at the second **QR code**.
* Tap the website link that appears on screen.
* In your browser, long-press the image and tap **Download image**.
* Check that the screenshot appears in your **Gallery**.
* And on your Switch, select **End** to disconnect.

And that's how you can easily send Nintendo Switch Lite screenshots to your Android phone!
