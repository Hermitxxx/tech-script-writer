Getting the "Not enough free space" error when downloading a new game on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the top fixes to free up space fast so your downloads can finish.
Let's get right into it!

SOLUTION 1: QUICK ARCHIVE UNPLAYED GAMES
* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap on **Quick Archive**.
* Check the boxes next to games you aren't playing.
* Move over to the right and select **Archive Data**.
* Confirm by choosing **Archive**.
* Keep in mind your save data stays completely safe.
* Now head back to your home screen.
* And check if your game download resumes.

Now, if you want to keep all your games installed, let's transfer some of them over to your microSD card.

SOLUTION 2: MOVE DATA TO MICROSD CARD
* From your home screen, open **System Settings**.
* Scroll down and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, select **Move Data Between System / microSD Card**.
* Choose **Move to microSD Card** from the options.
* Check the games or downloadable content you want to transfer.
* Move over to the right and click **Move Data**.
* Confirm by choosing **Move**.
* Give it a few moments to copy files over.
* And check your storage to confirm internal memory is freed.

If you still need extra space for that download, let's clear out large captured videos and screenshots.

SOLUTION 3: DELETE CAPTURED SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEOS
* First, head back to your home screen.
* Move over to the blue **Album** icon.
* Inside the album, press the " X " button.
* Check the boxes next to unwanted clips or screenshots.
* Move over to the right and select **Delete**.
* Confirm by choosing **Delete** once more.
* Now return to your home screen.
* And verify that your game download now starts downloading.

And that's how you can easily fix not enough free space on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
