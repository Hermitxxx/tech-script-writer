Wondering whether you should archive or delete a game on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you the exact difference and how to do both to free up storage space.
Let's get right into it!

METHOD 1: HOW TO ARCHIVE A GAME
* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap on **Manage Software**.
* Choose the specific game you want to archive.
* Click on **Archive Software**.
* Confirm by choosing **Archive**.
* Keep in mind your save data stays completely safe.
* Notice that the game icon stays on your home screen with a cloud icon.
* And check that you can tap the icon anytime to redownload.

Now, if you want to completely remove the game and declutter your home screen, let's permanently delete it.

METHOD 2: HOW TO DELETE A GAME
* From your home screen, open **System Settings**.
* Scroll down and select **Data Management**.
* On the right menu, tap on **Manage Software**.
* Choose the game you want to remove.
* Go ahead and select **Delete Software**.
* Confirm by choosing **Delete** once more.
* Keep in mind your save data is stored separately and won't be erased.
* And check your home screen to verify the game icon is gone.

And that's the difference between archiving and deleting games on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
