Are you getting the frustrating error code 2101-0001 on your Nintendo Switch Lite and can't use your console?
In this video, I'm gonna walk you through the top fixes to troubleshoot and resolve this error in minutes.
Let's get right into it!

FIX 1: PERFORM A FULL RESTART
* Press and hold the **POWER Button** for three seconds.
* Select **Power Options** from the popup menu.
* Go ahead and choose **Restart**.
* If your screen is frozen, hold the **POWER Button** for twelve seconds.
* Press the **POWER Button** once more to turn it back on.
* And verify that your Switch reboots cleanly to your home screen.

Now, if restarting didn't resolve the issue, let's disconnect any accessories and inspect your charging port.

FIX 2: INSPECT CHARGING PORT AND ACCESSORIES
* Unplug your charging cable, headphones, and any accessories.
* Check your **USB-C port** for dust or bent pins.
* Gently blow into the port to clear out loose debris.
* Plug your official Nintendo charger directly into a wall outlet.
* Leave your Switch plugged in for fifteen minutes without touching it.
* Press the **POWER Button** to turn the console on.
* And verify that the battery charging icon appears without the error.

Still running into that error code? Let's boot into Safe Mode to reset your system cache without deleting any save data.

FIX 3: SAFE MODE CACHE RESET
* Turn off your Nintendo Switch completely.
* Press and hold both **Volume Up** and **Volume Down** buttons.
* While holding volume, press the **POWER Button** once.
* Keep holding both volume buttons until **Maintenance Mode** appears.
* Select **Initialize Console Without Deleting Save Data**.
* Go ahead and click on **Next**.
* Confirm by choosing **Initialize Console Without Deleting Save Data**.
* Wait for the console to complete the reset.
* And verify that your console boots up smoothly without the error.

Now, if you've tried all of these solutions and the error code still won't go away, that points to a damaged power management chip on the motherboard, so you'll need to contact official Nintendo support to set up a repair.

And that's how you can troubleshoot error code 2101-0001 on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
