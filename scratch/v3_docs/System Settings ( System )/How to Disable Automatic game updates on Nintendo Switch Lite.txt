Want to stop your Nintendo Switch Lite from automatically updating games in the background?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to turn off auto-updates in just a few quick seconds.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll down the left sidebar and select **System**.
* On the right menu, look for **Auto-Update Software**.
* Go ahead and switch this setting to **Off**.
* This stops games from downloading updates in sleep mode.
* Now head back to your home screen.
* And verify that your setting is active.

And that's how you can easily disable automatic game updates on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
