Need to factory reset your Nintendo Switch Lite and start completely fresh?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to wipe all system memory in just a few simple steps.
Make sure you remove your microSD card first so its data stays safe, and let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down and select **System**.
* Now scroll to the bottom and choose **Formatting Options**.
* From here, scroll down and select **Initialize Console**.
* Review the warning screen on your display.
* Go ahead and click on **Next**.
* Click on **Next** once more on the popup.
* Make sure your console stays connected to the internet.
* Confirm the wipe by selecting **Initialize**.
* Give your console a few minutes to finish resetting.
* And verify that it boots into the initial setup screen.

And that's pretty much all there is to factory resetting your Nintendo Switch Lite!
