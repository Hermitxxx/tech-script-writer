Need to properly format a microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to format it directly from system settings in seconds.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down and select **System**.
* Scroll down to the bottom and select **Formatting Options**.
* From here, choose **Format microSD Card**.
* Review the warning and select **Continue**.
* Confirm by choosing **Format** to erase the card.
* Give your console a moment to restart automatically.
* Head into **Data Management** inside **System Settings**.
* And verify that your microSD card is active and ready.

And that's how you can properly format a microSD card on your Nintendo Switch Lite!
