Want to reset your Nintendo Switch Lite keyboard dictionary back to factory defaults?
In this video, I'm gonna show you how to quickly reset your keyboard settings in seconds.
Let's get right into it!

* First, let's head to your home screen.
* Move over to the **gear icon** for **System Settings**.
* Scroll all the way down and select **System**.
* Scroll to the very bottom and select **Formatting Options**.
* From here, click on **Reset Keyboard**.
* Review the prompt and go ahead and hit **Reset**.
* Keep in mind this only erases custom learned words.
* Tap on **OK** once the confirmation notice appears.
* And verify that your keyboard is back to default.

And that's pretty much all there is to resetting your keyboard settings on Nintendo Switch Lite!
