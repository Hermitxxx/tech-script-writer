Are your games suddenly lagging and dropping FPS on Windows 11?
Don't worry—in this video I'll show you the best settings to boost your performance, so let's get right into it!

STEP 1: DISABLE MEMORY INTEGRITY AND VBS
* First, press the windows key and " I " on your keyboard.
* Next, from the left menu, click on **Privacy & security**.
* Click right on **Windows Security**.
* From here, select **Device security**.
* Under core isolation, click on **Core isolation details**.
* Switch the **Memory integrity** toggle to **OFF**.
* When prompted, restart your PC to apply the change.

Now that virtualization overhead is turned off, let's enable Windows Game Mode so your system prioritizes gaming performance over background apps.

STEP 2: TURN ON WINDOWS GAME MODE
* First, open up your Windows **Settings** app.
* Next, from the left menu, click on **Gaming**.
* From the options, select **Game Mode**.
* Switch the toggle for **Game Mode** to **ON**.
* This prioritizes your CPU and GPU for gameplay.
* And prevents background downloads while playing games.

With Game Mode active, let's enable optimizations for windowed games right inside your graphics settings to reduce input latency and eliminate stutter.

STEP 3: TURN ON OPTIMIZATIONS FOR WINDOWED GAMES
* First, head back to your **Settings** app.
* From the left menu, click on **System**.
* Select **Display** from the list.
* Scroll down and click right on **Graphics**.
* Under default settings, locate **Optimizations for windowed games**.
* Switch the toggle right next to it to **ON**.
* This reduces input latency across all your games.
* And restart your game to apply the changes.

For our final optimization, let's stop Windows from throttling your processor by unlocking the High Performance power plan.

STEP 4: ENABLE HIGH PERFORMANCE POWER PLAN
* First, click on your **Start button**.
* Type " Control Panel " into the search box.
* From the results, click on **Control Panel**.
* Click right on **Hardware and Sound**.
* Next, select **Power Options** from the menu.
* Click the drop-down for **Show additional plans**.
* From here, select the **High performance** plan.
* And finally, close out of the window.

Your Windows 11 frame rates and overall gaming smoothness should now be noticeably improved!
