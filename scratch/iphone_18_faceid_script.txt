If your iPhone 18 Pro Max freezes and restarts after Face ID, you are definitely in the right place.
In this video, I'll walk you through the fastest and easiest fix to get it working again in no time.
Let's get right into it!

STEP 1: FORCE RESTART YOUR IPHONE
* First, quickly press and release **Volume Up**.
* Next, quickly press and release **Volume Down**.
* Now press and hold the **Side button**.
* Keep holding even if the power slider appears.
* Keep holding through the black screen.
* Release the **Side button** when the **Apple logo** appears.
* Let your iPhone reboot completely to your lock screen.
* Now test **Face ID** to confirm it unlocks smoothly.

Your iPhone 18 Pro Max should now be working completely back to normal!
