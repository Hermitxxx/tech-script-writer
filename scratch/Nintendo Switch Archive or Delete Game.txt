So let's jump right in!
METHOD 1: HOW TO ARCHIVE A GAME
•  First, highlight the game you want to archive on your HOME Menu.
•  Press the " + " button on your controller to open options.
•  Move down the left menu and open " Manage Software " .
•  Select " Archive Software " on the right side.
•  Select " Archive " to confirm.
•  This removes the game data to free up space while keeping your saved data safe.
•  You will Notice that the game icon stays on your HOME Menu with a small cloud download icon.
•  Finally, select the game icon anytime you want to quickly redownload it.
If you want to completely remove the game and declutter your home screen, here's how to delete it.
METHOD 2: HOW TO DELETE A GAME
•  First, highlight the game you want to delete on your HOME Menu.
•  Press the " + " button on your controller to open software options.
•  Scroll down and select " Manage Software ".
•  Select " Delete Software " from the menu.
•  Select " Delete " again to confirm the deletion.
•  You will Notice that both the game data and the game icon are now removed from your screen.
•  Finally, check your HOME Menu to confirm the icon is gone and your space is freed.
If you want to free up massive storage by archiving several games at once, let's use Quick Archive.
METHOD 3: BULK ARCHIVE MULTIPLE GAMES AT ONCE
•  First, go to your home screen.
•  Then Move over to the gear icon for " System Settings " and open it.
•  Scroll down the left sidebar and select " Data Management " .
•  On the right side, select " Quick Archive " .
•  Check the box next to any games you are not currently playing.
•  Look at the bottom of the screen to see the total storage space you will free up.
•  Select " Archive Data " on the right.
•  Select " Archive " to confirm.
•  Finally, return to your HOME Menu and check your newly available storage space.
And that's the difference between archiving and deleting games on your Nintendo Switch, and how to do both.
