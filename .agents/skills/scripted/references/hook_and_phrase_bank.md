# Scripted: Hook & Phrase Bank

This reference manual documents the proven formulas and phrasing patterns derived from analyzing the 373 docx tutorial scripts in `c:\SAAD SCRIPTS`.

---

## 1. Intro Hook Formulas (Short, Connected & High Retention)

Every video tutorial intro must be **ultra-short (2 to 3 lines max, 5 to 8 spoken seconds)**. Never delay the viewer with bloated paragraphs, lengthy medical-style cause descriptions, or redundant disclaimers. Establish the urgent pain point, provide immediate confidence, and launch straight into Step 1.

Rotate through these **5 dynamic archetypes** so every generated script stays fresh and distinct:

### Archetype 1: The Urgent Fix Hook (High Tension & Fast Relief)
- Line 1: `"Is your [Device/App] [symptom / frozen on logo / crashing]?"`
- Line 2: `"In this video, I'll walk you through the quickest ways to fix it and get it working again."`
- Line 3: `"Let's jump right in!"`

### Archetype 2: The Direct Multi-Step Fix Hook (Fast & Authoritative)
- Line 1: `"If your [Device/App] is [issue], here's a quick 3-step fix."`
- Line 2: `"Let's get right into it."`

### Archetype 3: The Symptom & Relief Hook (Empathetic & Punchy)
- Line 1: *"[Device/App] suddenly [issue] after an update or restart?"*
- Line 2: `"Here's the easiest way to solve the problem without losing any of your data."`
- Line 3: `"So, let's get started."`

### Archetype 4: The Curiosity / Teaser Hook (High Watch Urge)
- Line 1: `"Before you think your [Device] is completely dead, try this."`
- Line 2: `"Here's how to fix [Device/App issue] in just a few minutes."`
- Line 3: `"Let's dive in."`

### Archetype 5: The Rapid Launch Hook (Minimal & Zero Fluff)
- Line 1: `"Need to fix [issue] on your [Device] fast?"`
- Line 2: `"Here are the most effective solutions that actually work."`
- Line 3: `"Let's begin."`

## 2. Clickable UI Targets: Bold with Internal Spaces (`" Option "`)

Whenever any on-screen setting, button, menu item, hardware button, or app name that the user must tap or click is mentioned, it **must** be written in **Bold** and inside quotes with internal spaces:
- `**" Settings "**`
- `**" Storage "**`
- `**" Clear cache "**`
- `**" Force stop "**`
- `**" Digital Crown "**`
- `**" Side button "**`
- `**" Play Sound "**`

The generator script automatically renders these as native inline **bold** runs in the compiled `.docx` file.

---

## 3. Heading Conventions

Headings are styled **Bold**, `Aptos` 14pt (Sz=28) or 18pt (Sz=36).

### Standard Patterns:
1. **Troubleshooting**:
   - `SOLUTION 1: <ACTION IN ALL CAPS>` (e.g. `SOLUTION 1: CLEAR SYSTEM UI CACHE`)
   - `SOLUTION 2: <ACTION IN ALL CAPS>`
   - `SOLUTION 3: <ACTION IN ALL CAPS>`
2. **Alternative Methods**:
   - `METHOD 1: <ACTION IN ALL CAPS>` (e.g. `METHOD 1: BLOCK A CONTACT FROM A CHAT`)
   - `METHOD 2: <ACTION IN ALL CAPS>`
3. **Linear Walkthroughs / Features**:
   - `Step 1: <Action Title>`
   - `<FEATURE TITLE IN ALL CAPS>` (e.g. `EDIT YOUR MESSENGER USERNAME`)

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Cadence (One Action Per Line)

Each line represents exactly one spoken cue and screen tap.

### Chronological Signposts:
- Line 1: `First, open [App/Settings]...`
- Line 2: `Then, tap [Category]...`
- Line 3: `Now, select [Item]...`
- Line 4: `Next, find and tap [Option]...`
- Line 5: `Scroll down and tap [Setting]...`
- Line 6: `After that, tap [Action]...`
- Line 7: `Finally, check if [verification check]...`

### Precision Action Verbs:
- **Mobile**: `Tap`, `Press and hold`, `Swipe down from the top`, `Scroll down`, `Toggle on/off`, `Select`.
- **Desktop**: `Click`, `Move your cursor over`, `Press [Key combination]`, `Select`.

### Mandatory Verification Steps:
Every solution must end with an explicit check:
- `"Finally, check if the notification appears."`
- `"Reopen Chrome and test it again."`
- `"Send a test message and check if it delivers properly."`
- `"Check if WhatsApp Web connects properly."`
- `"Finally, check that your new username appears on your profile."`

---

## 4. Inter-Solution Failure Bridges

When moving from one solution to the next, insert a short conditional transition:

- `"If the problem is still there, move on to the next fix."`
- `"If the notification still doesn't appear, let's check whether Samsung is putting Messenger to sleep."`
- `"If your battery is still draining quickly, let's reduce unnecessary background activity."`
- `"If Chrome is already up to date but continues crashing, let's clear its temporary data."`
- `"If you can't find the conversation, there's another way to block the contact."`
- `"If nothing fixed the issue, you can try one final step."`

---

## 5. Outro Formulas

Outros follow a **3-sentence structure**:

### Line 1: Resolution Confirmation
- `"And that's it! Your [feature] should now be working normally on your [Device]."`
- `"And that's how you can fix the [Issue] problem on your [Device]."`
- `"That should resolve the problem."`
- `"Your Messenger username has now been updated."`

### Line 2: Reassurance / Benefit
- `"Hopefully, one of these methods helped you fix the problem."`
- `"After trying these solutions, your device should be able to connect normally again."`
- `"You can share your new username with others so they can easily find and message you."`

### Line 3: Sign-Off / Engagement CTA
- `"Thanks for watching, and I'll see you in the next video."` *(Top standard)*
- `"Thanks for watching, and I'll catch you in the next one!"`
- `"Thanks for watching, and I'll see you in the next one."`
- **YouTube CTA (when requested)**:
  `"If this video helped you, make sure to like, subscribe, and turn on notifications for more [Device] tips and easy troubleshooting guides. Thanks for watching, and I'll catch you in the next one!"`
