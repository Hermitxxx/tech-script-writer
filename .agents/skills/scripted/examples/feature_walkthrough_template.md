# Example 2: Feature Walkthrough / Multi-Method Guide

Use this format for tutorials that demonstrate how to customize or set up a feature.

---

### Raw Text Format

```text
Want to [perform task/customize feature] on your [Device]?
You can easily adjust this setting in just a few quick steps.
Let's get started.

METHOD 1: ADJUST FROM QUICK SETTINGS
First, swipe down from the top of your screen to open the Control Center.
Now, find the [Feature] toggle.
Tap the icon to turn it on or off.
Press and hold the icon to open its full settings menu.
Adjust the options according to your preference.
Finally, exit back to the home screen and check the result.

If you prefer to configure it through the main system settings, here's how.

METHOD 2: CONFIGURE THROUGH MAIN SETTINGS
First, open Settings on your [Device].
Scroll down and tap [Category].
Select [Feature].
Turn on the toggle switch.
Finally, test the feature to make sure it functions as expected.

And that's it! Your [Feature] settings are now completely configured.
Thanks for watching, and I'll see you in the next one.
```
